### What looks strange in the source

1. **The source was built from an earlier starter draft.** Its notes say "This strategy does not refine weapons in the starter catalogue" (Swarm, Trio, Tycoon), "Generalist refinement is reserved for the flagship" and "Dual-wield refinements affect the main-hand copy only". All three contradict approved decision 35 and R3.4. That is why most plans are empty and the rest put Hone/Augment before Sharpen/Forge.
2. **`validation-report.json` says PASS.** It checked against that earlier draft and lists profanity, the schema and STRATEGIES.md as not verified (A11).
3. **"Assembly" failed the substring profanity filter.** The old `containsProfanity` was a lower-case substring match, so 'ass' in "Assembly" failed four stables. Resolved by decision 1: under the whole-word rule of `bugfix/profanity-whole-word-matching` (merged to `main` in PR #528) all four pass, and so do robot names such as "Shell-Walker" or "Sea Brass" (robot names are not filtered anyway, V03). The collection stable names "HK Assassin Protocol", "Infinite War Assembly", "Kaylon Assembly Array", "Striker Assembly Wing", "Chimera City Scrapworks" and "Scrap Garden Restoration Shop" ('ass' or 'crap' inside a word) are in the same position and are kept under decision 1.
4. **Names and backstories are templated.** Team names follow "{Prefix} Pair 01 / Squad 01", Swarm robots are mostly "{Unit} 04 … 11" with identical art briefs, and every backstory is "{stable} enters the arena with a clear doctrine. {concept line}. Every surviving machine returns to the workshop before the next booking." The starter catalogue has franchise-flavoured names and backstories throughout.
5. **Two franchise labels are markdown residue**: "[Detroit" and "[One Must Fall" (the link text of "[Detroit: Become Human](…)" and "[One Must Fall: 2097](…)" was cut at the colon).
6. **`adminMaximumRobots` is wrong for most stables.** The engine maximum is min(highest robots bound, blueprints), for example 1 for a one-blueprint Paragon, not 2. The field is dropped, so this has no effect (A11, spec-55 impact 7).
7. **Seven `alternativeBlueprints`** describe a selection feature that does not exist.

The structural content is sound: weapons, loadouts, paths, teams and event orders all pass (see the last headline bullet above).

## 6. Manual content review (A09)

Every franchise label, stable name, robot name, alternative name and art brief of the review-record stables was read, and the collection's were keyword-checked (A09, including "portrayed by" likeness wording). Keyword hits are in the A09 details below; this is the reasoning.

- **No real persons, no real competition robots, no Minions.** Every robot is a fictional character or machine. The BattleBots precedent (CATALOGUE §1) is respected: Robot Wars and BattleBots do not appear. *Rise of the Robots*, *One Must Fall* and *Robocraft* are video games with fictional robots.
- **Performer likeness.** Several live-action androids are played by recognisable actors: Data, Lore and B-4 (*Star Trek*), David 8, Walter One and Bishop (*Alien*), Dolores, Maeve and Bernard (*Westworld*), Gigolo Joe and David (*A.I.*). Their art briefs ask for "human-looking" figures or "pale skin". Recommended: keep the characters, but the art brief should ask for a visibly synthetic design that is not a portrait of the actor. Sonny and the NS-5s (*I, Robot*) and the *Detroit* androids are CGI or game characters with a human-like face; lower risk, same recommendation.
- **"Gigolo Joe"** (*A.I.*) is a player-visible robot name with a sexual connotation (the character is a companion robot). Recommended: rename (for example "Joe"), or swap the slot for another *A.I.* mecha.

- **"Murder Drones"** is only the admin-only franchise label (the show's title); the stable name "Copper Nine Drone Works" is neutral. No change.
- **Westworld, Five Nights at Freddy's, Warhammer 40,000** are mature franchises, but the names and briefs are neutral. No change.
- **Decision 8 (README §4): kept.** Gigolo Joe, David Mecha and the human-looking android briefs stay as drafted, because the names pass the whole-word filter: `check-built.ts` finds no whole-word hit on any stable, team or robot name.
