# Requirements Document

## Glossary

- **Overview_Row**: The topmost substantive module on the player Dashboard (`app/frontend/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx`), sitting directly below the notification stack and above the Recent Battles section. Today it is a two-card grid holding `StableStatistics` ("Stable Overview") and `FinancialSummary` ("Financial Overview"). This spec replaces both cards with three tiles.
- **Dashboard_Tile**: The single shared presentational component used for all three tiles in the Overview_Row. Owns heading scale, container styling, stat-value colour rules, loading state, error state and click-through behaviour, so that no tile can drift from the others.
- **Prestige_Tile**: The first tile in the Overview_Row. Answers "am I climbing?" — current prestige total, prestige earned in the Current_Cycle, the Comparison_Figure for prestige, and progress toward the next Prestige_Gate.
- **Todays_Battles_Tile**: The second tile in the Overview_Row. Answers "has today happened yet, and how did it go?" — an aggregate only, never a per-battle list.
- **Credits_Tile**: The third tile in the Overview_Row. Answers "am I making or losing money, and where is it going?" — balance, Battle_Earnings, and Repair_Spend split into its automatic and manual `repairType` totals, each with its Comparison_Figure where one exists.
- **Current_Cycle**: The in-progress game cycle, measured from the most recent midnight UTC settlement boundary up to the moment of the request. The primary time basis for every changing figure in the Overview_Row.
- **Last_Completed_Cycle**: The most recent cycle for which a `cycle_snapshots` row exists. Snapshots are written by the Settlement_Job, so a snapshot exists only for a cycle that has finished.
- **Comparison_Figure**: The Last_Completed_Cycle total for a metric, rendered beside the Current_Cycle figure for the same metric so a player can read direction of travel.
- **Battle_Slot**: One of the nine daily times at which battle events run: 08:00 (1v1 League), 09:00 (2v2 League), 10:00 (1v1 Tournament), 11:00 (Tag Team), 13:00 (KotH), 14:00 (3v3 League), 15:00 (2v2 Tournament), 17:00 (Grand Melee) and 18:00 (3v3 Tournament) UTC. All nine are live and run daily, one round per cycle for the three tournament slots. The canonical map is `docs/architecture/PRD_SERVICE_DIRECTORY.md` § Cron Schedule, and the registrations are in `cycleScheduler.ts`. Credits, prestige and fame are awarded at battle completion inside each slot, not at settlement.
- **Outstanding_Match**: A match the player holds for the Current_Cycle whose Battle_Slot still lies ahead of the request timestamp — a `scheduled_matches_v2` row with status `'scheduled'` and a `scheduledFor` later than now, or a `scheduled_tournament_matches` row that is still pending with its mode's Battle_Slot later than now. The set of Outstanding_Matches supplies both the outstanding half of the scheduled count (Requirement 4 criterion 11) and the remaining Battle_Slot times (Requirement 4 criterion 3), so the two cannot disagree. A match whose slot has passed without producing a battle is not an Outstanding_Match; it leaves the count rather than sitting in it for the rest of the day, which is the lesser of two wrongs — the alternative renders an incomplete ratio alongside "all scheduled matches fought".
- **Match_Schedule_Source**: One of the two tables in which a queued match lives, as declared by `EVENT_SCHEDULE_SCOPES` in `app/backend/src/services/scheduling/eventScheduleScope.ts`. The six unified modes (`league_1v1`, `league_2v2`, `league_3v3`, `tag_team`, `koth`, `grand_melee`) queue rows in `scheduled_matches_v2` with participant rows in `scheduled_match_participants`. The three tournament modes (`tournament_1v1`, `tournament_2v2`, `tournament_3v3`) queue rows in `scheduled_tournament_matches`, which stores participants as two columns rather than as rows. Any count of scheduled matches that reads only one of the two sources is incomplete.
- **Settlement_Job**: The existing midnight UTC job that applies passive income and operating costs, closes the cycle, and writes the `cycle_snapshots` row. Invoked from `cycleScheduler.ts`.
- **Win_Loss_Mode**: A battle mode whose result is a win, a loss or a draw for the participating robot: every mode except those in `PLACEMENT_MODE_BATTLE_TYPES`.
- **Placement_Mode**: A battle mode whose result is a finishing position rather than a win or a loss. The set is `PLACEMENT_MODE_BATTLE_TYPES = ['koth', 'grand_melee']` in `userProfileService.ts`. Result position is stored per participant in `battle_participants.placement`.
- **Best_Placement**: The numerically lowest `battle_participants.placement` a player's robots achieved across all Placement_Mode battles in the Current_Cycle, reported together with the field size of that battle.
- **Reward_Earning_Placement**: A Placement_Mode finish that paid a reward. Grand Melee pays LP on the `GRAND_MELEE_LP_SCALE` (25/18/15/12/10/8/6/4/2/1) and fame to the top 10, and prestige to the top 3 only.
- **Battle_Earnings**: Credits a player's stable gained from combat activity in a cycle: battle credit awards plus streaming revenue. Excludes passive facility income and operating costs.
- **Repair_Spend**: Credits a player actually spent repairing robots in a cycle: the sum of `cost` across both `repairType` values in Repair_Spend_Source. A manual repair event carries `cost` reduced by the Manual_Repair_Discount and, until Requirement 18 is implemented, reduced a second time by the Repair_Bay_Discount that the Repair_Quote had already applied — so a manual `cost` written today is not the credits the player was charged. The same manual event carries `preDiscountCost`, intended as the same repair priced before the Manual_Repair_Discount and after the Repair_Bay_Discount, and today likewise carrying that discount twice. An automatic repair event carries `cost` at full price after the Repair_Bay_Discount, and carries no `preDiscountCost`. Once Requirement 18 is in force, the automatic `cost` and the manual `preDiscountCost` share one basis, both being post-Repair_Bay_Discount and pre-Manual_Repair_Discount; that shared basis is the state Requirement 18 establishes, not a property of the code today, because the Manual_Repair_Path currently applies the Repair_Bay_Discount a second time when it writes both of its figures. In neither state is `preDiscountCost` an input to a spend total: summing it would report manual spend at double what was charged and drop automatic spend entirely. Avoidable_Repair_Spend is derived from the automatic total alone, so its correctness does not depend on the manual understatement being fixed.
- **Manual_Repair_Discount**: The 50% reduction a player receives by repairing a robot before its next scheduled match rather than letting the pre-battle cron repair it. Declared once, as `MANUAL_REPAIR_DISCOUNT = 0.5` in `app/shared/utils/repairCost.ts`; the Backend reaches that same declaration through the `app/backend/src/shared/utils` symlink rather than through a second copy. Applied after the Repair_Bay_Discount, never before it. After this spec the constant is declared once, in the Shared_Repair_Module.
- **Avoidable_Repair_Spend**: *Retired by the amendment of 26 August 2026 and no longer rendered.* Defined as automatic Repair_Spend × 0.5 — the credits a player would have kept by repairing manually. The Credits_Tile now shows the automatic and manual `repairType` totals as two plain lines instead. The automatic line answers the same question more directly, because it is money actually spent at full price rather than a hypothetical saving needing a sentence of label to explain. The term is kept here so that the reasoning behind its removal survives, and because `docs/prd_pages/PRD_INCOME_DASHBOARD.md` may still choose to derive it.
- **Repair_Spend_Source**: The single authoritative source for all repair spend figures: `audit_logs` rows with `eventType: 'robot_repair'`, whose payload carries `cost`, `repairType`, `manualRepairDiscount` and, on manual events only, `preDiscountCost`.
- **Prestige_Gate**: A prestige threshold that unlocks a facility level. Thresholds and helpers live in `app/shared/utils/prestigeGates.ts` (`getUnlockedFacilityLevel`, `getNextPrestigeThreshold`).
- **Cycle_Progress_Summary**: The response of the new authenticated read endpoint that aggregates everything the Overview_Row needs for the Current_Cycle: battles fought, matches scheduled, win/loss/draw counts, Best_Placement, Battle_Earnings, and Repair_Spend split by `repairType`.
- **Lifetime_Stat**: A figure covering a stable's entire history rather than a bounded period. On the Overview_Row today: `highestELO`, `highestLeague`, `highestTagTeamLeague`, `totalRobots`, `totalBattles`, lifetime wins, lifetime losses, lifetime draws and lifetime win rate.
- **Income_Dashboard**: The existing page at `/income` that carries the full financial breakdown the Credits_Tile links out to.
- **Booking_Office**: The existing per-robot event subscription system that gates participation in all battle events and therefore determines how many matches a stable has scheduled for a given day.
- **Cycle_Scheduler**: The existing daily cycle orchestration in `cycleScheduler.ts`.
- **Preparation_Phase**: The existing 2-cycle window between competitive seasons during which no battles are scheduled (Spec #45).
- **Season_Rollover**: The existing season boundary operation that archives and then purges `audit_logs`, `cycle_snapshots`, `battles` and related history (Spec #45).
- **Standing**: The existing `standings` table, the single source of truth for competitive ranking data including LP.
- **Same_Stable_Pairing**: A single battle in which the player holds robots on both sides — two of their own robots meeting in `league_1v1`, or two of their own teams meeting in a team mode. Matchmaking discourages rather than prevents it: `calculateMatchScore` in `services/matchmaking/teamMatchmakingUtils.ts`, the shared scoring function all league modes use, adds `SAME_STABLE_PENALTY` of 10000 to a score whose other terms are typically in the hundreds, which its own comment describes as "effectively blocks unless no other option". A thin tier or instance with no alternative pairing therefore still produces one. The Placement_Modes use a different mechanism for the same purpose — `resolveStableConflicts` swaps robots between groups in `kothMatchmakingService` and `grandMeleeMatchmakingService`, also best-effort, with a comment stating the conflict can remain — but a Placement_Mode battle yields no win, loss or draw, so a Same_Stable_Pairing has no effect on the outcome counts there.
- **Frontend**: The React application under `app/frontend/src/`, including its components, hooks, pages and tests.
- **Backend**: The Express application under `app/backend/src/`, including its routes, services, types and tests.
- **Shared_Repair_Module**: `app/shared/utils/repairCost.ts` — the one module in which the repair cost formula, the Repair_Bay_Discount, the Manual_Repair_Discount constant and the rounding rules are declared, imported by both Frontend and Backend. Placement is mandated by `.kiro/steering/coding-standards.md`, which requires a formula shared between frontend and backend to live in `app/shared/utils/` and never be locally redefined.
- **Repair_Quote**: The credits required to repair a robot to full at its current damage, after the Repair_Bay_Discount and before the Manual_Repair_Discount. Computed as `sumOfAllAttributes × 100 × (damagePercent / 100) × Damage_Multiplier × (1 - Repair_Bay_Discount)`, rounded to whole credits. This is the figure an automatic repair charges and the figure the Frontend shows as an estimate.
- **Damage_Multiplier**: The severity factor applied to a Repair_Quote: `2.0` at 0% HP, `1.5` below 10% HP, `1.0` otherwise.
- **Repair_Bay_Discount**: The Repair Bay facility reduction applied to a Repair_Quote, computed as `min(90, repairBayLevel × (5 + activeRobotCount)) / 100`, capped at 90%.
- **Charged_Repair_Cost**: The credits actually deducted from a player for repairing one robot: the Repair_Quote on the Automatic_Repair_Path, and the Repair_Quote reduced by the Manual_Repair_Discount on the Manual_Repair_Path.
- **Manual_Repair_Path**: The player-initiated repair flow — `robotRepairService.repairAllRobots`, reached from `routes/robots.ts`.
- **Automatic_Repair_Path**: The pre-battle cron repair flow — `repairService.repairRobots`, reached via `repairRobotsForEvent`.
- **Repair_Cost_Parity_Test**: The test that pins the Charged_Repair_Cost produced by the Shared_Repair_Module against the amounts the pre-consolidation `calculateRepairCost` in `app/backend/src/utils/robotCalculations.ts` produced, across every Damage_Multiplier boundary and across Repair Bay levels including the 90% cap, so that single-sourcing is demonstrably behaviour-preserving rather than assumed to be.
- **Repair_Audit_Parity_Test**: The test that pins each Repair_Spend_Source figure written by the Manual_Repair_Path against the credits actually deducted and against the Repair_Quote, at Repair Bay levels above 0 and at the 90% Repair_Bay_Discount cap, and that pins the Automatic_Repair_Path figures as unchanged, so that a second application of the Repair_Bay_Discount cannot be reintroduced without a test failing.
- **Repair_Ledger_Entry**: A `financial_ledger` row with `transactionType: 'repair_cost'` and a negative amount equal to a Charged_Repair_Cost, written through `recordLedgerEntry` from `app/backend/src/services/financial/recordLedgerEntry.ts`.
- **Cycle_Battle_Export**: The per-cycle battle CSV produced by `app/backend/src/services/cycle/cycleCsvExportService.ts`, one row per battle participant, whose header currently declares a `repair_cost` column populated from `payload.repairCost`.
- **Cached_Repair_Quote**: The `robots.repairCost` column — a stored Repair_Quote for the robot's current damage, zeroed on repair. Both repair paths recompute the quote and ignore this column; it exists to let a list view show an estimate without recomputing.
- **Lifetime_Repair_Spend**: The `robots.totalRepairsPaid` column — cumulative credits actually charged for repairing that robot across its whole life, incremented by both repair paths.
- **Cycle_Repair_Spend**: The `totalRepairCosts` key inside the `stableMetrics` JSON of a `cycle_snapshots` row — credits actually charged for repairs for one stable during that one cycle.
- **Repair_Figure_Store**: One of the four persisted repair figures, each retained because each answers a different question: Repair_Spend_Source (what happened, per event), Lifetime_Repair_Spend (what a robot has cost in total), Cached_Repair_Quote (what a repair would cost right now), Cycle_Repair_Spend (what repairs cost this stable this cycle).

## Introduction

The Overview_Row is the first substantive thing a player sees after logging in, and it currently answers almost nothing about the day in front of them. It leads with Lifetime_Stats that move once a month or never (`highestELO`, `highestLeague`, `totalBattles`, lifetime win rate), duplicates the credit balance already shown in the navigation bar, and renders that duplicate in a different colour. Of the 13 figures the row renders today, only 2 — the prestige delta and the win/loss cycle delta — can differ between a player's first and fourth login on the same day.

This spec replaces the two existing cards with three tiles, ordered left to right: Prestige_Tile, Todays_Battles_Tile, Credits_Tile. Each answers exactly one question, all three are built from one shared Dashboard_Tile component, and every changing figure is stated on the Current_Cycle basis with the Last_Completed_Cycle as its Comparison_Figure.

The spec also fixes where repair figures come from, because the Credits_Tile reports two of them. Establishing the source turned up more than one loose end, and all of them are in scope here rather than deferred.

The first is a dead field read in two places. `cycleSnapshotService.aggregateStableMetrics` reads `payload.repairCost` off `battle_complete` events, but `logBattleAuditEvent` in `battlePostCombat.ts` never writes that field and no orchestrator injects it. The read type-checks only because `CycleEventPayload` declares `repairCost?: number`. Snapshot totals are correct today by accident: the moment any orchestrator adds `repairCost` to its extras, every stable's repair total silently doubles and the Income_Dashboard starts misreporting, with no change at the read site to explain it. The second read site is not latent at all. `cycleCsvExportService.ts` populates the `repair_cost` column of the Cycle_Battle_Export from the same missing field, so that column has exported `0` on every row since it was added — a wrong number an admin can download today.

The second is three declarations of the repair cost formula. `app/shared/utils/repairCost.ts` is the Shared_Repair_Module and exports `calculateRepairCost`. `app/backend/src/utils/robotCalculations.ts` exports a second `calculateRepairCost`, and that is the one both repair paths actually execute. `app/frontend/src/components/YieldThresholdSlider.tsx` holds a third, inline and non-exported, recomputing the base cost, the Damage_Multiplier and the Repair_Bay_Discount for its scenario table.

Note what is *not* a fourth copy: `app/backend/src/shared/utils` is a **symlink** to `app/shared/utils`, so `app/backend/src/shared/utils/repairCost.ts` and `app/shared/utils/repairCost.ts` are one file reached by two paths. The Backend already imports the Shared_Repair_Module through that symlink for `MANUAL_REPAIR_DISCOUNT`. The symlink is undocumented anywhere in the repository and is why this arrangement reads as more duplication than it is.

The header comment of the Shared_Repair_Module documents the duplication in its own words, instructing that any change in `robotCalculations.ts` must be mirrored there "until the backend is migrated to import from this shared module". That migration never happened. So the authoritative number — what the player is actually charged — runs on the declaration that is not the shared one, and the estimate the player reads on screen comes from two further declarations that nothing enforces agreement with. `.kiro/steering/coding-standards.md` already forbids this arrangement.

The third is a ledger gap. `financialService.ts` declares twelve `TransactionType` values and six are written today: `battle_income`, `weapon_purchase`, `weapon_sale`, `weapon_refinement`, `facility_upgrade` and `robot_creation`. `repair_cost` is declared and never written, so a cost every fighting stable incurs daily is absent from the transaction record.

The fourth is naming. Four separate stores hold repair figures and all four earn their place, but `robots.repairCost` reads as money spent when it is a quote, `totalRepairCosts` on a per-cycle metric reads as a lifetime figure, and the audit payload's `cost` does not say whether it is charged or quoted — the one distinction the Manual_Repair_Discount turns into two different numbers.

The fifth is not an ambiguity but a wrong number, and it is the one that makes the Credits_Tile figures wrong rather than merely fragile. `POST /repair-all` in `routes/robots.ts` applies the Repair_Bay_Discount a second time when it builds the audit payload: it computes `perRobotCostAfterRepairBay = Math.floor(robot.calculatedRepairCost * (1 - result.discount / 100))` and then halves that for the charged figure, but `robot.calculatedRepairCost` is a Repair_Quote that `calculateRepairCost` has already discounted — `robotRepairService` says so itself, in the comment "Repair bay discount is already baked into calculatedRepairCost via calculateRepairCost()". The credits deducted from the player are right and the Lifetime_Repair_Spend increment is right, because both are computed in the service from the quote with one discount. Only the two audit figures are wrong, and Repair_Spend_Source is the one place every repair total is read from. At a level-2 Repair Bay with 5 active robots the discount is 20%, so every manual row records 80% of what the player paid; at the 90% cap it records a tenth. `/admin/repair-log` has been under-reporting manual spend for as long as the code has stood, Cycle_Repair_Spend sums the same figure so per-cycle snapshot totals are understated too, and the Income_Dashboard inherits that. The Automatic_Repair_Path is unaffected: `repairService` logs `event.repairCost`, which carries one discount.

These belong in one spec with the Overview_Row redesign for a plain reason: the Credits_Tile's whole justification is that Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend respond to whether the player showed up. Putting that number in the most prominent position on the Dashboard while it rests on three copies of a formula, a dead field and a spend category the ledger never sees means shipping a headline figure no single line of code is answerable for. Fixing it now is cheaper than shipping the tile and reconciling afterwards.

## Design Decisions and Rejected Alternatives

These decisions are settled. They are recorded here because the reasoning is not recoverable from the code, and because a future change to the Overview_Row will otherwise re-litigate them.

### The time basis is Current_Cycle, with Last_Completed_Cycle as the comparison

Credits, prestige and fame are incremented at battle completion in `battlePostCombat.ts` (`awardCreditsToUser`, `awardPrestigeToUser`, `awardFameToRobot`). Only passive income and operating costs wait for the Settlement_Job. A player's money and prestige therefore move up to nine times a day, once per Battle_Slot, which makes "this cycle so far" a real and moving figure rather than a rounding of the daily total.

Rejected alternatives:

- **Rolling 24 hours.** Figures shrink between refreshes as events age out of the window. A module whose job is to show momentum cannot have its numbers go down because time passed.
- **Since last login.** `lastLoginAt` is written at login as a fire-and-forget update in `routes/auth.ts`, so by the time the Dashboard's requests land it is already approximately "now" and the window is empty. The codebase has already hit this: the tier-changes endpoint in `routes/leagues.ts` uses a fixed 24h window specifically to dodge the same race. That precedent rules the approach out rather than inviting a second workaround.
- **Last_Completed_Cycle only.** Static for a whole day. A player logging in for the second, third or fourth time sees identical numbers in the most prominent position on the page.
- **A rolling multi-cycle average as the Comparison_Figure.** Steadier, but less legible. Subscriptions change rarely, so a large day-over-day swing is a real signal about what happened yesterday, not noise worth smoothing away.

The tier-change notification keeps its fixed 24h window. This is deliberate and not an inconsistency: a tier change is an event, shown once, that either happened or did not. A total is a quantity that must be attributable to a defined period, so it needs a period with edges.

### Outcome representation is mode-specific

A single W–L figure misrepresents Placement_Mode results, which is why `userProfileService.ts` already excludes `PLACEMENT_MODE_BATTLE_TYPES` when counting wins and losses. Grand Melee resolves by placement across up to 20 robots and pays LP and fame to the top 10 and prestige to the top 3, so a placement-built robot can have a good day without winning anything. The Overview_Row must be able to say that. Per-battle LP delta is not available: `battle_participants` carries `eloBefore`, `eloAfter`, `prestigeAwarded`, `credits`, `finalHP` and `placement`, while LP lives on Standing. No figure in the Overview_Row may depend on per-battle LP.

### Repair lines beat settlement lines on the Credits_Tile

Passive income and operating costs were considered for the Credits_Tile and rejected on value-per-line grounds. They are settlement figures, they barely move day to day, and the Income_Dashboard already presents them properly. Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend earn their lines because they are the only figures on the row that respond to whether the player showed up.

### The tile carries numbers, the notification carries the button

Repair action stays in the existing notification stack (priority 4, "damaged robots"). The Credits_Tile states the cost and the avoidable portion; it does not offer a repair button. Separately, the existing damaged-robots notification should state both the manual and the automatic price so the Manual_Repair_Discount is visible at the point of action — that change is out of scope for this spec.

### Automatic repair is deadline-driven, not settlement-driven

Automatic pre-battle repair is scoped per cron to that event's own participants (`services/economy/repairScope.ts`), specifically so a player has a window in which to take the Manual_Repair_Discount. A robot's auto-repair deadline is its next scheduled match, not midnight. Copy on the Credits_Tile must not imply that repairs are charged at settlement.

### The formula consolidation happens here, not in a follow-up spec

The two tracked declarations of the repair cost formula agree today — whether the inline copy in `YieldThresholdSlider.tsx` agrees is unverified, which is itself the argument — so a follow-up spec was considered. It was rejected. Nothing enforces the agreement, the file that documents the duplication has been asking for the migration since it was written, and this spec is about to promote repair figures to the most prominent module on the Dashboard. A deferred cleanup on a formula that two independent execution paths and one on-screen estimate all depend on is a cleanup that gets deferred again.

### The four Repair_Figure_Stores stay; only their names change

Each of the four answers a different question and none is redundant: Repair_Spend_Source is the event record and the only auditable one, Lifetime_Repair_Spend is a per-robot running total, Cached_Repair_Quote is a forward-looking estimate rather than a spend at all, and Cycle_Repair_Spend is a per-cycle rollup consumed by the Income_Dashboard. Collapsing any pair would mean deriving one of those answers on every read.

What is wrong is the naming. `robots.repairCost` reads as money spent when it is a quote for money not yet spent. `totalRepairCosts` on a per-cycle metric reads as a lifetime figure. The audit payload's `cost` does not say whether it is the amount charged or the amount quoted, which is exactly the distinction the Manual_Repair_Discount turns into two different numbers. Requirement 17 renames them and states the migration cost of each so design can weigh it, rather than leaving a reader of this code to work the distinction out from the call sites a third time.

### Only `repair_cost` is added to the ledger

Five other declared `TransactionType` values are also never written: `streaming_revenue`, `subscription_cost`, `prestige_award`, `attribute_upgrade` and `settlement_adjustment`. They are out of scope. `repair_cost` is in scope because this spec is already establishing the authoritative repair total and `routes/robots.ts` already imports `recordLedgerEntry` for `robot_creation`, so the Manual_Repair_Path has the helper in the same file. The other five are named here so their omission is a recorded decision rather than an oversight.

Note what this does and does not change: `financial_ledger_active` in `services/migration/featureFlags.ts` defaults to `false`, and `financialService.recordTransaction` returns `null` when the flag is off. Writing Repair_Ledger_Entries therefore produces no observable change to the Income_Dashboard or anywhere else until that flag is enabled. This is consistency work that makes the ledger correct whenever it is switched on, not a visible feature.

### No historical correction of the understated manual repair figures

Requirement 18 fixes the manual audit figures going forward and corrects nothing already written. That is a decision, not an omission.

A pre-fix row cannot be repaired from its own contents. Recovering the true charged figure needs the Repair_Bay_Discount that applied at the moment of the repair, which is the Repair Bay level and the active robot count as they stood then. The payload carries `discountPercent`, but the row carries no robot attribute totals, and the two inputs that produced the discount are mutable: a player upgrades the bay, buys robots and sells them. Reconstructing the discount for an arbitrary past row therefore means reconstructing a history the database does not keep.

The rows also age out on their own. `audit_logs` is pruned under `AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` and purged entirely at Season_Rollover, so the population a backfill could touch shrinks daily and reaches zero at the next season boundary. Writing a correction script whose input set expires is worse than accepting the understatement and saying so.

So no backfill is attempted, and no new correction mechanism is introduced. Pre-fix Repair_Spend_Source rows keep their understated values and the Cycle_Repair_Spend totals derived from them stay understated. The consequence a reader must expect is a discontinuity in the manual repair series at the cycle the fix ships, not a corrected history.

### `backfillCycleSnapshots` stays create-only, and the correction promise is withdrawn

An earlier draft of Requirement 9 criterion 8 said a wrong stored `cycle_snapshots` repair total could be corrected by re-running `adminCycleService.backfillCycleSnapshots`. That was not true of the code. The operation skips any cycle that already has a snapshot, and `cycleSnapshotService.createSnapshot` calls `prisma.cycleSnapshot.create` against a `@unique` `cycleNumber`, so it cannot rewrite an existing row — it would fail the uniqueness constraint if it tried.

Two options were weighed. The first was to give the operation a reprocess path guarded by a surviving-source check, which would have made the promise real at the cost of an upsert path on `createSnapshot`. The second was to withdraw the promise and leave the operation alone.

The second was chosen, for three reasons. Players have no visibility of the discrepancy, so nothing is owed to them. This is an ACC environment, where the value of a corrected history is low. And a one-off correction script is itself a liability: it is code that exists to run once, is tested less than production code, and complicates the operation it touches for everyone who reads it afterwards.

The objective is therefore narrower and firmer: **everything is correct from the moment this spec lands on ACC.** No historical figure is rewritten, `backfillCycleSnapshots` and `createSnapshot` keep their existing create-only behaviour, and Requirement 9 criteria 8 and 11 state the prohibition rather than a correction procedure.

## Expected Contribution

1. **Figures that can change within a day: 2 → 12.** Today only the prestige delta and the win/loss cycle delta differ between two logins on the same day. After this spec, every figure on the Overview_Row except the four Comparison_Figures and the Prestige_Gate threshold moves within a single day.
2. **Nine Lifetime_Stats removed from the Dashboard.** `highestELO`, `highestLeague`, `highestTagTeamLeague`, `totalRobots`, `totalBattles`, lifetime wins, lifetime losses, lifetime draws and lifetime win rate leave the Overview_Row. They need no new home: per-robot detail pages and league standings already carry them.
3. **The duplicated credit balance in a second colour is eliminated.** `FinancialSummary` currently renders the balance in `text-success` green while the navigation bar renders the same number as a cyan `₡` figure. After this spec there is one balance styling on the page, and green retains its "healthy" meaning instead of being spent on a neutral number.
4. **Two dead reads removed, not one, and repair spend single-sourced.** `payload.repairCost` disappears from `cycleSnapshotService.aggregateStableMetrics`, from `cycleCsvExportService.ts` and from `CycleEventPayload`, closing the double-count trap in the first and a live wrong value in the second. ## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Overview_Row Composition and Order

**User Story:** As a player logging in, I want the first module on my Dashboard to tell me where I stand today, so that I know within one glance whether I need to act.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Overview_Row SHALL render exactly three tiles, in document order Prestige_Tile first, Todays_Battles_Tile second, Credits_Tile third, so that the row reads Prestige, Today's Battles, Credits left to right in the three-column grid and top to bottom when stacked.
2. THE Overview_Row SHALL render as the immediate sibling following the notification stack and the immediate sibling preceding the Recent Battles section, with no other Dashboard module between it and either neighbour, and SHALL hold that position regardless of how many notifications the notification stack renders, including zero.
3. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render only the current prestige total, prestige earned in the Current_Cycle, the Comparison_Figure for prestige, and progress toward the next Prestige_Gate, and SHALL NOT render any figure outside that set.
4. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render only battles fought in the Current_Cycle against matches scheduled for the Current_Cycle, the Win_Loss_Mode win, loss and draw counts for the Current_Cycle, the Best_Placement with the field size of that battle, and the upcoming Battle_Slot times, and SHALL NOT render any figure outside that set.
5. THE Credits_Tile SHALL render only the current credit balance, Battle_Earnings, Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend, each with its Comparison_Figure where one exists, and SHALL NOT render any figure outside that set.
6. THE Overview_Row SHALL NOT render any row, figure or label attributable to a single identified battle, with the single exception of the Best_Placement figure and its field size, because per-battle detail is the responsibility of the Recent Battles section below it.
7. THE Overview_Row SHALL NOT render any control that initiates or confirms a robot repair, because repair action remains in the notification stack.
8. IF the Cycle_Progress_Summary read has not yet resolved or returns an error, THEN THE Overview_Row SHALL render the same three tiles in the same order, each in its Dashboard_Tile loading or error state, so that tile count and tile order never depend on data availability.
9. IF every figure belonging to one tile is omitted under Requirement 10, THEN THE Overview_Row SHALL still render that tile with its heading and an explanatory empty state, and SHALL NOT drop the tile from the row or reorder the remaining tiles.

### Requirement 2: Current_Cycle Basis with Last_Completed_Cycle Comparison

**User Story:** As a player who logs in several times a day, I want the numbers to reflect what has happened since midnight, so that they change as my robots fight.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Overview_Row SHALL compute every changing figure over the Current_Cycle, defined as the half-open interval beginning at the most recent midnight UTC settlement boundary inclusive and ending at the timestamp of the request exclusive, and SHALL capture that boundary pair once per request so that every figure on the row covers the identical interval.
2. THE Overview_Row SHALL render exactly four Comparison_Figures — the Last_Completed_Cycle totals for prestige earned, Battle_Earnings, Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend — each rendered within the same tile as, and adjacent to, its Current_Cycle counterpart, with at most one Comparison_Figure per Current_Cycle figure.
3. THE Overview_Row SHALL source each Comparison_Figure for prestige and Battle_Earnings from the single `cycle_snapshots` row having the highest cycle number strictly lower than the cycle number of the Current_Cycle.
4. THE Overview_Row SHALL source each Comparison_Figure for Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend from Repair_Spend_Source rows whose timestamp falls within the Last_Completed_Cycle window, defined as the half-open interval beginning at the midnight UTC boundary that opened that cycle inclusive and ending at the midnight UTC boundary that closed it exclusive.
5. THE Overview_Row SHALL attach to every rendered figure a period label drawn from exactly two values, one naming the Current_Cycle and one naming the Last_Completed_Cycle, such that the count of rendered figures carrying no period label is zero.
6. THE Overview_Row SHALL NOT compute any figure over a window defined as the request timestamp minus a fixed duration, including a 24-hour, 7-day or any other backwards-measured span.
7. THE Overview_Row SHALL NOT read `lastLoginAt` for any purpose, including window boundaries, figure computation and period labels, and SHALL NOT derive any window from a per-user login timestamp.
8. IF no `cycle_snapshots` row exists for the cycle immediately preceding the Current_Cycle at the time of the request, because the Settlement_Job has not yet written it, THEN THE Overview_Row SHALL treat the most recent existing `cycle_snapshots` row as the Last_Completed_Cycle, SHALL scope the Repair_Spend and Avoidable_Repair_Spend Comparison_Figures to that same cycle's window, and SHALL label those Comparison_Figures with the cycle they cover rather than with the immediately preceding cycle.
9. IF reading the Last_Completed_Cycle sources fails, THEN THE Overview_Row SHALL render every Current_Cycle figure with its period label unchanged, SHALL omit the four Comparison_Figures, and SHALL render an indication that the comparison is unavailable, distinct from a zero value.

### Requirement 3: Prestige_Tile

**User Story:** As a player, I want to see my prestige, what I gained today, and how far I am from the next facility unlock, so that I can tell whether today moved me toward a new capability.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the authenticated player's current stable prestige total as a whole number of 0 or greater.
2. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render prestige earned during the Current_Cycle as a whole number, and SHALL render `0` when no prestige was earned in the Current_Cycle, because a Current_Cycle total of zero is a known figure rather than an absent one.
3. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render prestige earned during the Last_Completed_Cycle as the Comparison_Figure, and SHALL omit the Comparison_Figure and any direction indicator when no `cycle_snapshots` row exists for the Last_Completed_Cycle.
4. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the prestige remaining to the next Prestige_Gate as `required` minus the current prestige total, taking `required` from `getNextPrestigeThreshold` called with that same total, together with a progress bar whose filled proportion is the current prestige total minus the `PRESTIGE_GATES` threshold of the currently unlocked facility level, divided by `required` minus that same threshold, expressed as a whole percentage clamped to the range 0 to 100.
5. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the remaining-prestige figure and the progress-bar percentage as text alongside the bar, so that the bar is not the only carrier of the value.
6. IF `getNextPrestigeThreshold` returns `null` for the current prestige total, THEN THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the facility level returned by `getUnlockedFacilityLevel` for that total, which is 10 in this case, in place of both the remaining figure and the progress bar.
7. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL obtain the unlocked facility level, a value in the range 3 to 10, from `getUnlockedFacilityLevel` called with the current prestige total, rather than reimplementing the threshold comparison.
8. IF the Current_Cycle prestige figure is lower than the Comparison_Figure and the Comparison_Figure is present, THEN THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the signed difference between the two figures with a decline indication, and SHALL NOT clamp that difference to zero or omit it, because prestige moves only on wins and top-3 Placement_Mode finishes and is never reduced by spending.
9. IF the Current_Cycle prestige figure is equal to or greater than the Comparison_Figure and the Comparison_Figure is present, THEN THE Prestige_Tile SHALL render the difference without the decline indication.
10. THE Frontend SHALL re-read the authenticated player's prestige total from the server when the Dashboard mounts, by invoking the `refreshUser` function `AuthContext` already exposes, so that the prestige total, the Current_Cycle prestige figure and the Prestige_Gate progress derived from that total all describe the same moment. `AuthContext` calls `refreshUser` once when the application mounts and not again unless asked, so without this criterion a player who navigates within the single-page application across a Battle_Slot boundary and then opens the Dashboard would see a stale total beside a current Current_Cycle figure, and a Prestige_Gate progress bar computed from the stale total.
11. THE Prestige_Tile SHALL continue to render the prestige total, the Current_Cycle figure and the gate progress if the `refreshUser` re-read fails, using the prestige total already held in the auth context, and SHALL NOT enter the Dashboard_Tile error state on that failure, because a stale total is more useful than no tile.

### Requirement 4: Todays_Battles_Tile

**User Story:** As a player with several robots subscribed to several events, I want one aggregate view of today's fighting, so that I can tell whether the day has run without reading twenty battle rows.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render battles fought in the Current_Cycle against matches scheduled for the Current_Cycle as a single fought-of-scheduled figure with the fought count first, summed across all battle modes together with no per-mode breakdown, each count rendered as a whole number from 0 to 999 without abbreviation.
2. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL take both counts from the Cycle_Progress_Summary, which derives the scheduled count from matches the Booking_Office has already placed on the schedule — knowable at login because matchmaking books exactly one cycle ahead via `defaultScheduledFor()` in `services/matchmaking/teamMatchmakingUtils.ts` — counting each scheduled match once per stable regardless of how many of the player's robots take part in it.
3. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render the earliest two distinct Battle_Slot times, in ascending chronological order, at which the player has at least one match scheduled and not yet fought within the Current_Cycle, each labelled as a UTC time of day, counting a slot once however many of the player's matches fall inside it.
4. WHILE more than two such Battle_Slots remain in the Current_Cycle, THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render the earliest two followed by a "+N more" indicator, where N is the count of remaining distinct Battle_Slots beyond those two and is an integer from 1 to 7, because Battle_Slot defines exactly nine daily times.
5. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL NOT render credits or prestige for any battle, because those figures belong to the Credits_Tile and the Prestige_Tile.
6. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render aggregate figures only, SHALL NOT render a list of individual battles, and SHALL NOT render more Battle_Slot times than the two required above plus the "+N more" indicator, however many matches the stable has scheduled.
7. IF the Cycle_Progress_Summary read fails or returns no Current_Cycle data, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render the shared Dashboard_Tile error state indicating that today's figures are unavailable, and SHALL NOT render a zero fought count, a zero scheduled count or any Battle_Slot time.
8. **Superseded by criterion 12 (amendment of 26 August 2026).** This criterion originally read: *"IF the fought count exceeds the scheduled count, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render both counts as returned and SHALL NOT render a completion proportion greater than 100 percent."* Rendering the counts as returned was the wrong instruction — it sanctioned `4 of 1`, which was then observed on screen. See criteria 11 and 12.
9. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL count matches from both Match_Schedule_Sources in the scheduled count, and SHALL count battles from every battle mode in the fought count, so that the two halves of the fought-of-scheduled figure cover the same set of modes. Reading only `scheduled_matches_v2` would omit all three tournament modes from the scheduled count while the fought count continued to include their battles, and because the 1v1 Tournament, 2v2 Tournament and 3v3 Tournament Battle_Slots all run daily, the figure would report more fought than scheduled on an ordinary day rather than in the edge case criterion 8 anticipates.
10. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL draw the remaining Battle_Slot times of criterion 3 from both Match_Schedule_Sources, so that a pending tournament round is never omitted from the upcoming-slot line.
11. THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL derive the scheduled count as the fought count plus the count of Outstanding_Matches, so that the fought count is a subset of the scheduled count by construction and the rendered ratio cannot exceed 100 percent whatever the schedule rows contain. THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL NOT derive the scheduled count by counting schedule rows, because the two Match_Schedule_Sources cannot be filtered symmetrically: `scheduled_matches_v2` rows carry a `scheduledFor` and a status, while a `scheduled_tournament_matches` row carries neither a scheduled time nor any link to the battle it produced. THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL draw Outstanding_Matches from the same set that supplies the remaining Battle_Slot times of criterion 3, so that the scheduled count and the upcoming-slot line cannot disagree.
12. IF the fought count exceeds the scheduled count, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render the fought count alone and SHALL NOT render the ratio. This is unreachable while criterion 11 holds and exists so that a future regression degrades to a less precise figure rather than an impossible one: a ratio like `4 of 1` in the most prominent module on the Dashboard discredits every other figure beside it.
13. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL label its win/loss line and its placement line each with the count of fought battles it covers, and THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL report those two counts such that they sum to the fought count. A record of `2W 0L 0D` beneath a fought count of 4 is otherwise unreadable: nothing on the tile says that the missing two battles were Placement_Modes reported on the line below, and a new player has no way to infer that KotH and Grand Melee resolve by finishing position rather than by win or loss. THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL NOT derive either count from `winLossDraw`, because a Same_Stable_Pairing is one battle carrying both a win and a loss, so the outcome total can exceed the battle count.

### Requirement 5: Mode-Appropriate Outcome Display

**User Story:** As a player running a placement-built robot, I want a 6th-of-20 finish shown as the result it is, so that the Dashboard does not tell me I lost when I earned rewards.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render Win_Loss_Mode results in the Current_Cycle as three separate integer counts — wins, losses and draws — counting **one result per distinct `(battleId, team)` pair** on which the player has at least one `battle_participants` row, so that a 3v3 League or 3v3 Tournament battle contributes exactly one result rather than three, matching the once-per-stable rule Requirement 4 criterion 2 applies to the scheduled count and Requirement 4 criterion 1 applies to the fought count. Counting one result per participant row would report three wins against one battle fought for a single 3v3 victory.
2. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL exclude battles whose mode appears in `PLACEMENT_MODE_BATTLE_TYPES` from the win, loss and draw counts.
3. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render Placement_Mode results in the Current_Cycle as the Best_Placement in ordinal form together with the field size of the battle that produced it, the field size being the count of `battle_participants` rows for that battle, for example "best 3rd of 20".
4. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL derive Best_Placement from `battle_participants.placement`, which is populated for both `'koth'` and `'grand_melee'`.
5. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL NOT apply the loss colour or the error colour to any Placement_Mode result, regardless of the position achieved.
6. IF the Best_Placement is a Reward_Earning_Placement, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render it with a visual treatment that differs from the treatment used for a non-earning placement in at least one rendered attribute other than the placement text itself, so that the player can tell whether the finish paid.
7. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL classify a placement of 1 to 3 as prestige-earning, a placement of 1 to 10 as LP-and-fame-earning, and a placement of 11 or greater as non-earning when determining whether a placement is a Reward_Earning_Placement, applying the same position bands to `'koth'` and `'grand_melee'` and to every field size.
8. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL NOT render any per-battle LP figure, including any point value read from `GRAND_MELEE_LP_SCALE`, because per-battle LP delta is not persisted and LP lives on Standing.
9. IF two or more Placement_Mode battles in the Current_Cycle produced the same numerically lowest placement for the player's robots, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL report that placement with the largest field size among those battles, so that the rendered placement-and-field pair is deterministic.
10. IF a Placement_Mode battle in the Current_Cycle records no placement for any of the player's participating robots, THEN THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL exclude that battle from the Best_Placement derivation and SHALL derive Best_Placement from the remaining Placement_Mode battles.
11. THE Backend SHALL determine the outcome of a `(battleId, team)` pair by reading `battles.winningSide` first, treating the pair as a win when `team` equals that value and a loss otherwise; WHERE `winningSide` is null, THE Backend SHALL fall back to `battles.winnerId`, treating the pair as a win when a `battle_participants` row for that battle and team holds that robot id and a loss otherwise; and WHERE both `winningSide` and `winnerId` are null, THE Backend SHALL treat the pair as a draw. The fallback is required rather than optional because `winningSide` is documented in the schema as null for both a draw and a 1v1, so it cannot distinguish those two cases alone.
12. THE Backend SHALL derive every outcome from `battles.winningSide`, `battles.winnerId` and `battle_participants.team` only, and SHALL NOT read `battle_log` for any outcome, because `battle_log` is NULLed seven days after the battle under the retention rule established by Spec #39 while all three of those columns survive.
13. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render win, loss and draw counts whose sum equals the fought count of Requirement 4 criterion 1 for every Current_Cycle in which the player holds robots on at most one side of each battle, and SHALL render a sum greater than the fought count only in a Same_Stable_Pairing, where the player holds robots on both sides of one battle. In that case the battle contributes one win and one loss, which is the informative reading and the reason the grouping key is `(battleId, team)` rather than `battleId` alone. The two figures sit on separate lines and the tile SHALL NOT render any text asserting that the outcome counts sum to the fought count.
14. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL apply the once-per-`(battleId, team)` rule of criterion 1 without special-casing any Win_Loss_Mode, so that a Same_Stable_Pairing in `league_1v1`, `league_2v2`, `league_3v3`, `tag_team`, `tournament_2v2` or `tournament_3v3` is treated identically, and SHALL NOT exclude a Same_Stable_Pairing from the outcome counts, because a battle excluded from the tile above while appearing in the Recent Battles section below would contradict it.
15. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL count a Placement_Mode battle containing two or more of the player's robots as one battle fought and one match scheduled, and SHALL contribute no win, loss or draw for it under criterion 2, so that several of the player's robots in one KotH or Grand Melee field collapse to a single Best_Placement figure under criterion 3 rather than to multiple results.

### Requirement 6: Credits_Tile

**User Story:** As a player, I want to see what I earned today and what repairs cost me, split between the repairs I caught myself and the ones the cron paid full price for, so that I have a reason to check in before my robots fight.

> **Amendment of 26 August 2026 — repair spend is split by type, not totalled and derived.**
> Criteria 3 and 4 originally specified one Repair_Spend total plus an
> Avoidable_Repair_Spend figure derived as `automatic × 0.5`. As built, that rendered as
> `Repairs ₡7,468` above `Avoidable — repair before each robot's next match ₡0`: three
> numbers to reconcile, a label long enough to wrap, and a headline figure that was not a
> transaction the player could find anywhere else. The split below carries the same
> message with less text, because an automatic repair IS a repair the player was not
> present for, charged at full price. Criteria 3 and 4 are replaced by 3a and 3b;
> criteria 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11 are amended in place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Credits_Tile SHALL render the authenticated player's credit balance as at the time of the request, rounded to whole credits, and SHALL render it without a Comparison_Figure because a balance is a point-in-time value rather than a period total.
2. THE Credits_Tile SHALL render Battle_Earnings for the Current_Cycle, computed as battle credit awards plus streaming revenue and excluding passive facility income and operating costs, alongside the Last_Completed_Cycle total as its Comparison_Figure.
3. **(3a)** THE Credits_Tile SHALL render the automatic `repairType` total from Repair_Spend_Source for the Current_Cycle as its own line, labelled as automatic repairs, alongside the Last_Completed_Cycle automatic total as its Comparison_Figure.
4. **(3b)** THE Credits_Tile SHALL render the manual `repairType` total from Repair_Spend_Source for the Current_Cycle as its own line, labelled as manual repairs, alongside the Last_Completed_Cycle manual total as its Comparison_Figure. THE Credits_Tile SHALL NOT render a combined repair total and SHALL NOT render a derived avoidable figure: the two lines are the breakdown, and a third number computed from them is one more thing to explain.
5. THE Credits_Tile SHALL render each repair line from its own `repairType` total only, and SHALL NOT read `creditsBeforeManualDiscount` into either line. Both lines therefore report credits actually charged, and neither depends on the automatic and manual figures sharing a basis — which is what makes them correct both before and after the Requirement 18 fix.
6. THE Credits_Tile SHALL treat a Current_Cycle Battle_Earnings figure above its Comparison_Figure as favourable and an automatic repair figure above its Comparison_Figure as unfavourable, and SHALL treat the manual repair figure as having no meaningful direction, so that no colour is applied to it. Every credit on the automatic line is a credit the player would have halved by showing up, so a rise there is unambiguously bad; manual spend rises both when a player fights more and when they start catching repairs they previously missed, so rendering a rise in the error colour would flag the behaviour the Manual_Repair_Discount exists to encourage.
7. THE Credits_Tile SHALL render a link to the Income_Dashboard at `/income`, labelled to indicate that the full financial breakdown is there.
8. THE Credits_Tile SHALL NOT render passive facility income or operating costs, which remain the responsibility of the Income_Dashboard.
9. THE Credits_Tile SHALL label the two repair lines by `repairType` alone, with no explanatory clause attached to either. The deadline for taking the Manual_Repair_Discount is documented in the in-game guide articles `economy/repair-costs.md` and `combat/yielding-and-repair-costs.md`, which state that it is a robot's next scheduled match; a tile label is not where that belongs. THE Credits_Tile SHALL NOT describe repair as charged at settlement or at midnight.
10. IF the Cycle_Progress_Summary read fails or returns no body, THEN THE Credits_Tile SHALL render the Dashboard_Tile error state in place of the Battle_Earnings and the two repair lines, SHALL continue to render the credit balance and the Income_Dashboard link, and SHALL indicate that today's figures could not be loaded.
11. THE Backend SHALL satisfy Requirement 18 before either repair figure reports the credits players were charged, because both criteria 3a and 3b assume the Repair_Spend_Source charged figure equals the amount deducted, and the Manual_Repair_Path reduced that figure by the Repair_Bay_Discount a second time before that fix — understating the manual line specifically, and so flattering the contrast a player draws between the two.
12. THE Frontend SHALL re-read the authenticated player's credit balance from the server when the Dashboard mounts, through the same single `refreshUser` invocation Requirement 3 criterion 10 requires, so that the balance, Battle_Earnings and Repair_Spend on the tile all describe the same moment, and SHALL NOT issue a second request for the balance alone.
13. THE Credits_Tile SHALL continue to render the balance and the Income_Dashboard link if the `refreshUser` re-read fails, using the balance already held in the auth context, consistent with criterion 10's requirement that both survive a failed read.

### Requirement 8: Cycle_Progress_Summary Endpoint

THE Cycle_Progress_Summary service SHALL derive the matches-scheduled count and the remaining Battle_Slot times from both Match_Schedule_Sources, and SHALL determine which source applies to a mode by reading `EVENT_SCHEDULE_SCOPES` from `services/scheduling/eventScheduleScope.ts` rather than by declaring a second list of modes or hard-coding either table, so that adding a tenth event mode cannot silently omit it from either figure.

THE Cycle_Progress_Summary SHALL treat a `scheduled_tournament_matches` row as scheduled for the Current_Cycle when its status is `pending` or `scheduled` and it belongs to a tournament whose status is `active`, matching the filter `resolveOutstandingEventsForRobots` already applies, and SHALL resolve a team-participant bracket row to every member of that team so a team tournament round is counted once per stable rather than once per robot.

### Requirement 9: Repair_Spend Single-Sourcing and Dead Payload Read Removal

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want exactly one source for repair spend, so that adding a field to a battle payload cannot silently double every stable's repair total, and so that no export can publish a repair figure nothing writes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Backend SHALL source every repair figure returned by the Cycle_Progress_Summary, rendered on the Overview_Row, or stored as `totalRepairCosts` on a `cycle_snapshots` row from Repair_Spend_Source only, and SHALL NOT derive any repair figure from a `battle_complete` payload or from the `robots.repairCost` column.

3. THE Backend SHALL remove the `repairCost` field from `CycleEventPayload` in `app/backend/src/types/snapshotTypes.ts`, so that reintroducing the dead read fails to compile.

THE Cycle_Progress_Summary service SHALL aggregate Repair_Spend by fetching only the `audit_logs` rows with `eventType: 'robot_repair'` that belong to the authenticated user and whose event timestamp falls inside the requested cycle window, then summing `cost` per `repairType` value in application code or by raw SQL, because Prisma cannot `_sum` a field inside a JSON column.

6. THE Backend SHALL count both the manual path in `routes/robots.ts` and the automatic path in `services/economy/repairService.ts` toward total Repair_Spend, and SHALL derive Avoidable_Repair_Spend from the automatic `repairType` `cost` total alone.

WHERE a stored `cycle_snapshots` repair total was produced by the removed `battle_complete` read, THE Backend SHALL leave that total uncorrected, and SHALL NOT introduce a migration, a one-off correction script, or a reprocess path on `adminCycleService.backfillCycleSnapshots`. The operation cannot correct such a total today — it skips any cycle that already has a snapshot, and `cycleSnapshotService.createSnapshot` calls `create` against a `@unique` `cycleNumber` — and this spec deliberately does not give it that ability.
9. WHEN `cycleSnapshotService.aggregateStableMetrics` computes `totalRepairCosts` for a cycle, THE Backend SHALL sum `cost` across the Repair_Spend_Source rows whose event timestamp falls inside that cycle's window, preserving the field the existing aggregation already reads, and SHALL record zero for a stable with no such row.

THE Backend SHALL retain `adminCycleService.backfillCycleSnapshots` as a create-only operation with its existing skip-if-a-snapshot-exists guard, SHALL retain `cycleSnapshotService.createSnapshot` as create-only, and SHALL add no reprocess path, no upsert and no skipped-cycle reporting field to either. A cycle whose Repair_Spend_Source rows have been deleted by the audit log retention job under a configured `AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` window, or purged by Season_Rollover, is therefore never revisited and its stored repair total is never rewritten.

THE Backend SHALL remove the `repair_cost` column from the Cycle_Battle_Export entirely — from the header string, from the row interface and from the row builder — rather than repointing it at another source, because a Cycle_Battle_Export row is one battle participant and a Repair_Spend_Source row carries no battle reference, so no repair figure can be attributed to an identified battle. The removed column has emitted `0` on every row of every export produced since it was introduced.
14. THE Backend SHALL emit a Cycle_Battle_Export header naming exactly eleven columns, being the twelve columns emitted today with `repair_cost` absent and the relative order of the remaining eleven unchanged, and SHALL emit each data row with eleven comma-separated values in that same order.
15. THE Backend SHALL NOT populate any Cycle_Battle_Export column with a stable-level or cycle-level repair total repeated across that stable's battle rows, because a per-cycle figure repeated on each of a stable's rows sums to a multiple of the true figure for any consumer that totals the column.
16. WHERE the Cycle_Battle_Export is later extended with a repair figure, THE Backend SHALL source that figure from Repair_Spend_Source and SHALL state the period the figure covers in the column name.
17. THE Backend SHALL satisfy Requirement 18 before the Repair_Spend totals defined in criteria 1, 4, 6 and 9 report the credits players were charged, because every one of those criteria treats the Repair_Spend_Source charged figure as the amount deducted and the Manual_Repair_Path reduces that figure by the Repair_Bay_Discount a second time today, so single-sourcing this requirement establishes a correct source only once that second application is removed.

10. THE Todays_Battles_Tile SHALL render a battles-fought count of zero against the matches-scheduled count as a value rather than an omission, because zero fought against a known scheduled count is a known figure rather than an absent one.
11. WHEN more than one omission condition in this requirement applies at the same time, THE Overview_Row SHALL render the no-robots prompt in place of all Todays_Battles_Tile figure lines, and SHALL apply every other omission independently line by line.

### Requirement 15: One Repair Cost Implementation

#### Acceptance Criteria

THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL compute a Repair_Quote as the sum of all robot attributes multiplied by 100, multiplied by the damage percentage divided by 100, multiplied by the Damage_Multiplier, multiplied by one minus the Repair_Bay_Discount, and SHALL round that product to the nearest whole credit, reproducing the arithmetic all three current copies perform.
5. THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL compute the Repair_Bay_Discount as the Repair Bay level multiplied by the sum of 5 and the active robot count, capped at 90, divided by 100.
6. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL obtain each robot's Repair_Quote from the Shared_Repair_Module function and SHALL NOT compute a repair price by any other means.
7. THE Automatic_Repair_Path SHALL obtain each robot's Repair_Quote from the Shared_Repair_Module function and SHALL NOT compute a repair price by any other means.
8. THE Frontend SHALL obtain every repair figure it renders from the Shared_Repair_Module, retaining the existing `app/shared/utils/repairCost.ts` import in `app/frontend/src/hooks/useRobotsList.ts`, and SHALL declare no repair cost arithmetic of its own; specifically THE Frontend SHALL remove the local non-exported `calculateRepairCost` declaration and its accompanying attribute-sum loop from `app/frontend/src/components/YieldThresholdSlider.tsx` and obtain that component's scenario figures from the Shared_Repair_Module instead.
9. THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL apply the Manual_Repair_Discount in exactly one exported function, and THE Backend SHALL reach the discounted amount only by calling that function, so that no call site multiplies a cost by `MANUAL_REPAIR_DISCOUNT` directly.
10. THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL round a Repair_Quote to the nearest whole credit and SHALL round a manually discounted Charged_Repair_Cost down to a whole credit, preserving the `Math.round` and `Math.floor` behaviour each path applies today.
11. WHERE a manual repair covers two or more robots, THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL apply the Manual_Repair_Discount to each robot's Repair_Quote and then sum the results, and SHALL use that sum as the amount deducted from the player, the amount summed into the Lifetime_Repair_Spend increments, and the amount recorded in Repair_Spend_Source, so that all four figures agree to the credit.
12. WHERE a manual repair covers N robots, THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL charge the per-robot-derived total defined in criterion 11 even where that total is up to N minus 1 credits lower than the batch-level `Math.floor(totalBaseCost × MANUAL_REPAIR_DISCOUNT)` figure charged today, because the two roundings the current code performs — one on the batch total for the deduction and one per robot for the `totalRepairsPaid` increment — can disagree with each other, and the per-robot figure is the one that reconciles with the per-robot audit, lifetime and ledger records.
13. THE Repair_Cost_Parity_Test SHALL assert that the Shared_Repair_Module reproduces the Charged_Repair_Cost the pre-consolidation `calculateRepairCost` in `app/backend/src/utils/robotCalculations.ts` produced, for cases covering a robot at 0% HP, a robot above 0% and below 10% of maximum HP, a robot above 10% of maximum HP, an undamaged robot, Repair Bay level 0, a Repair Bay level and active robot count whose product is below the 90% cap, and a Repair Bay level and active robot count whose product exceeds the 90% cap.
14. THE Repair_Cost_Parity_Test SHALL compare each case against an expected value captured from the pre-consolidation implementation and recorded in the test, and SHALL NOT derive an expected value by calling the consolidated function, so that the test can fail.
15. THE Repair_Cost_Parity_Test SHALL assert that a manual repair of two or more robots charges the sum of the per-robot Charged_Repair_Costs, covering criterion 11.
16. IF a robot is at full HP, THEN THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL return a Repair_Quote of 0 and THE Backend SHALL deduct no credits, record no Repair_Spend_Source row and write no Repair_Ledger_Entry for that robot.
17. IF the Repair Bay level, the active robot count or an attribute total supplied to the Shared_Repair_Module is negative or not a finite number, THEN THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL signal an error to the caller rather than returning a negative or non-finite Repair_Quote.

### Requirement 16: Repair Credits in the Financial Ledger

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want repairs recorded in the transaction ledger like every other credit movement, so that the ledger is not silently missing a player's largest recurring outgoing when it is switched on.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL write a Repair_Ledger_Entry through `recordLedgerEntry` with `transactionType: 'repair_cost'`, an amount equal to the negation of the Charged_Repair_Cost, and a `balanceAfter` equal to the player's credit balance after the repair deduction.
2. THE Automatic_Repair_Path SHALL write a Repair_Ledger_Entry through `recordLedgerEntry` with `transactionType: 'repair_cost'`, an amount equal to the negation of the Charged_Repair_Cost, and a `balanceAfter` equal to the player's credit balance after the repair deduction.
3. THE Backend SHALL write one Repair_Ledger_Entry per Repair_Spend_Source row, at the same granularity that source uses, so that the two records reconcile one to one for any cycle in which both exist.
4. THE Backend SHALL invoke `recordLedgerEntry` after the repair's database transaction has committed, and SHALL NOT enrol the ledger write in that transaction, following the pattern `routes/robots.ts` already uses for `robot_creation`.
5. IF the Repair_Ledger_Entry write fails for any reason, THEN THE Backend SHALL leave the repair committed, SHALL return the repair response unchanged, and SHALL surface no error to the caller.
6. THE Backend SHALL carry a description on each Repair_Ledger_Entry that names the repair path as manual or automatic and the count of robots the entry covers, so that a ledger reader can tell the two paths apart without joining to `audit_logs`.
7. WHILE the `financial_ledger_active` flag in `services/migration/featureFlags.ts` is `false`, THE Backend SHALL persist no `financial_ledger` row for a repair, because `financialService.recordTransaction` returns `null` when the flag is off, and SHALL return the identical repair response it returns today, so that adding Repair_Ledger_Entries changes nothing a player or an admin can observe until that flag is enabled.
8. WHILE the `financial_ledger_active` flag is `true`, THE Backend SHALL record `repair_cost` amounts for a cycle whose sum equals the negation of the Repair_Spend for that same cycle derived from Repair_Spend_Source.
9. THE Backend SHALL continue to source every repair figure rendered on the Overview_Row, returned by the Cycle_Progress_Summary or stored as Cycle_Repair_Spend from Repair_Spend_Source, per Requirement 9 criterion 1, and SHALL NOT read `financial_ledger` for any of them.
10. THE Backend SHALL add ledger writes for the `repair_cost` transaction type only, leaving the five other declared-but-unwritten `TransactionType` values — `streaming_revenue`, `subscription_cost`, `prestige_award`, `attribute_upgrade` and `settlement_adjustment` — out of scope for this spec, so that their absence remains a recorded decision.

### Requirement 17: Explicit Names for Repair Figures and Repair Functions

**User Story:** As a maintainer reading a repair figure for the first time, I want its name to tell me the period it covers and whether it is money charged or money quoted, so that I do not have to read the call sites to find out.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Backend SHALL rename the Cached_Repair_Quote Prisma field and its mapped column from `repairCost` to a name that states the value is a quote for a repair not yet performed rather than credits already paid, `repairQuoteCredits` being the recommendation for the design phase to confirm. The migration cost is a Prisma migration plus every read and write site of the column, and there is no compatibility window to manage because the value is a cache that both repair paths recompute and ignore.
2. THE Backend SHALL rename the Lifetime_Repair_Spend Prisma field and its mapped column from `totalRepairsPaid` to a name that states the value is lifetime credits charged for that robot, `lifetimeRepairCreditsPaid` being the recommendation for the design phase to confirm. The migration cost is a Prisma migration plus every read and increment site, and the value carries across the rename unchanged.
3. THE Backend SHALL rename the Cycle_Repair_Spend key inside the `stableMetrics` JSON of `cycle_snapshots` from `totalRepairCosts` to a name that states the value is credits charged during that one cycle, `cycleRepairCreditsPaid` being the recommendation for the design phase to confirm. There is no Prisma migration because the key lives inside a `Json` column, and consequently every `cycle_snapshots` row written before the rename retains the old key.
4. THE Backend SHALL rename the Repair_Spend_Source payload key `cost` to a name that states the value is credits actually charged, `creditsCharged` being the recommendation for the design phase to confirm, and SHALL rename `preDiscountCost` to a name that states the value is the same repair priced before the Manual_Repair_Discount and after the Repair_Bay_Discount, `creditsBeforeManualDiscount` being the recommendation for the design phase to confirm. There is no Prisma migration because both keys live inside the `audit_logs` payload, and consequently every row written before the rename retains the old keys.
5. THE Backend SHALL leave the Repair_Spend_Source payload keys `repairType` and `manualRepairDiscount` unrenamed, so that the existing `payload.repairType` filter behind `GET /api/admin/audit-log/repairs` keeps matching rows written both before and after this spec.
6. THE Shared_Repair_Module SHALL name its two exported functions so that one states it returns a quote after the Repair_Bay_Discount and before the Manual_Repair_Discount and the other states it applies the Manual_Repair_Discount, `calculateRepairQuote` and `applyManualRepairDiscount` being the recommendations for the design phase to confirm, and SHALL NOT export two functions whose names differ only by a qualifier such as `calculateRepairCost` alongside `calculateRobotRepairCost`.
7. THE Backend SHALL express each Prisma field rename in criteria 1 and 2 as a column rename migration, and SHALL NOT add a second column and leave the original in place.
8. THE Backend SHALL read Cycle_Repair_Spend by resolving the renamed key first and falling back to `totalRepairCosts` when the renamed key is absent from a `stableMetrics` payload, because Requirement 9 criterion 11 leaves a stored total unchanged when its source rows have been purged and therefore a pre-rename row cannot always be re-derived.
9. WHEN `cycleSnapshotService.aggregateStableMetrics` or `adminCycleService.backfillCycleSnapshots` writes a `stableMetrics` payload, THE Backend SHALL write the renamed Cycle_Repair_Spend key only, and SHALL NOT write both keys.
10. THE Backend SHALL read the Repair_Spend_Source charged and pre-discount figures by resolving the renamed keys first and falling back to `cost` and `preDiscountCost` when the renamed keys are absent, in `getRepairAuditLog` in `adminSystemStatsService.ts` and in the Cycle_Progress_Summary repair aggregation, so that rows written before the rename and still inside the `AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` window continue to report their true amounts.
11. THE Backend SHALL write the renamed Repair_Spend_Source keys only on new `robot_repair` audit rows, and SHALL NOT write the old keys alongside them.
12. THE Backend SHALL carry, at each fallback introduced by criteria 8 and 10, a comment naming Season_Rollover as the condition under which the fallback becomes removable, because Season_Rollover purges both `cycle_snapshots` and `audit_logs` and therefore no pre-rename row survives it.
13. THE Frontend SHALL update `app/frontend/src/pages/admin/RepairLogPage.tsx` in the same change as `getRepairAuditLog`, and SHALL render the same columns, the same filters and the same values for a pre-rename row as it renders today.
14. THE Backend SHALL preserve the value of every renamed figure across the rename, such that a robot's Lifetime_Repair_Spend, a stable's Cycle_Repair_Spend and an audit row's charged amount each report the same number before and after this spec.
15. WHERE Requirement 9 and Requirement 6 name the Repair_Spend_Source payload fields `cost` and `preDiscountCost`, THE Backend SHALL treat those references as naming the same two fields under whichever names criterion 4 puts in force, and the rename SHALL change no figure those requirements define.
16. IF a `stableMetrics` payload or a `robot_repair` payload carries both the old and the renamed key for the same figure, THEN THE Backend SHALL use the renamed key and SHALL NOT sum the two, so that a partially migrated row cannot double a repair total.

### Requirement 18: Correct the Manual Repair Audit Figures

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want a manual repair audit row to state the credits the player was actually charged, so that the admin repair log, the per-cycle repair total and the Credits_Tile report repair spend rather than a fraction of it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL record, for each robot repaired, a Repair_Spend_Source charged figure equal to the credits deducted from the player for that robot, that figure being the Charged_Repair_Cost as Requirement 15 criterion 11 defines it.
2. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL record, for each robot repaired, a Repair_Spend_Source pre-discount figure equal to that robot's Repair_Quote unmodified, the Repair_Quote already carrying the Repair_Bay_Discount.
3. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL apply the Repair_Bay_Discount exactly once across the computation of both figures named in criteria 1 and 2, that single application being the one the Shared_Repair_Module performs when it produces the Repair_Quote.
4. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL remove the expression `Math.floor(robot.calculatedRepairCost * (1 - result.discount / 100))` from the audit logging loop in `routes/robots.ts`, SHALL pass `robot.calculatedRepairCost` as the pre-discount argument to `eventLogger.logRobotRepair`, and SHALL obtain the charged argument from the Shared_Repair_Module function that applies the Manual_Repair_Discount, per Requirement 15 criterion 9.
5. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL NOT apply `result.discount`, the Repair_Bay_Discount percentage that `robotRepairService.repairAllRobots` returns, to any figure derived from `robot.calculatedRepairCost`, because that field already carries the Repair_Bay_Discount, as the comment "Repair bay discount is already baked into calculatedRepairCost via calculateRepairCost()" in `robotRepairService.ts` states.
6. THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL continue to deduct the credits Requirement 15 criteria 11 and 12 define and SHALL continue to increment Lifetime_Repair_Spend by the same per-robot figure, because both already apply the Repair_Bay_Discount once and neither is changed by this requirement.
7. THE Backend SHALL carry a Repair_Audit_Parity_Test asserting that, for a robot repaired through the Manual_Repair_Path at a Repair Bay level above 0, the Repair_Spend_Source charged figure equals the credits deducted for that robot and the Repair_Spend_Source pre-discount figure equals that robot's Repair_Quote.
8. THE Repair_Audit_Parity_Test SHALL derive each expected value from the Repair_Quote and the Manual_Repair_Discount alone, and SHALL NOT derive an expected value from the logged payload or from the handler under test, so that the test fails against the implementation in place before this requirement.
9. THE Repair_Audit_Parity_Test SHALL include a case whose Repair Bay level and active robot count give a Repair_Bay_Discount of 90 percent, being the cap Requirement 15 criterion 5 sets and the point at which a second application of the discount records one tenth of the charged amount.
10. THE Automatic_Repair_Path SHALL record the Repair_Spend_Source charged figure it records today, being the `event.repairCost` value `services/economy/repairService.ts` passes to `eventLogger.logRobotRepair`, and SHALL record no pre-discount figure, and THE Repair_Audit_Parity_Test SHALL assert both, because that path applies the Repair_Bay_Discount once already.
11. WHERE a manual repair covers two or more robots, THE Manual_Repair_Path SHALL record Repair_Spend_Source charged figures whose sum equals the credits deducted for that batch, which holds because criterion 1 records the same per-robot figure that Requirement 15 criterion 11 sums to produce the deduction, and THE Repair_Audit_Parity_Test SHALL assert that equality for a batch of two or more robots.
12. THE Backend SHALL leave every Repair_Spend_Source row written before this requirement is implemented at its recorded values, SHALL attempt no backfill of those rows, and SHALL add no migration or one-off script that rewrites them, because `audit_logs` ages out under `AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` and is purged in full at Season_Rollover, and because the Repair Bay level and active robot count that produced a past row's Repair_Bay_Discount are not recoverable from the row itself.
13. THE Backend SHALL leave the Cycle_Repair_Spend total of every cycle whose snapshot was built from pre-fix rows understated by the same factor, SHALL NOT treat `adminCycleService.backfillCycleSnapshots` as a correction for those cycles, and SHALL introduce no other correction mechanism, because re-running that operation over a cycle whose Repair_Spend_Source rows survive recomputes the same understated sum from those same rows, and over a cycle whose rows have been purged it leaves the stored total unchanged and reports the cycle as skipped under Requirement 9 criterion 11.
14. WHEN this requirement is implemented, THE Credits_Tile SHALL render a Repair_Spend figure for a given cycle that is equal to or greater than the figure the same cycle would have produced beforehand, and the Avoidable_Repair_Spend figure of Requirement 6 criterion 4 SHALL be unchanged by this requirement because it derives from the automatic total alone; a Repair_Spend figure that differs across the fix for one cycle is the corrected value rather than a regression.
15. WHERE Requirement 17 criterion 4 renames the Repair_Spend_Source payload keys `cost` and `preDiscountCost`, THE Backend SHALL treat every reference in this requirement to the charged figure and the pre-discount figure as naming those two keys under whichever names that criterion puts in force.
