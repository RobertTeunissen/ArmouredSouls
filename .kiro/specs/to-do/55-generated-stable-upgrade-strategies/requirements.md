# Requirements Document

# Requirements: Generated Stable Upgrade Strategies

## Glossary

- **Generated_Stable** — a server-maintained account identified by `User.isGenerated = true`; its season-owned state is archived before the live account may be removed at `Season_Rollover`, after which a fresh generated stable is reseeded for the new season rather than applying the human reset contract.
- **Human_Stable** — a real-user account identified by `User.isGenerated = false`; it continues through the existing human reset contract at `Season_Rollover`.
- **Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program** — the server-owned program that seeds and economically progresses selected `Generated_Stable` accounts.
- **Maintenance_Profile** — a versioned, server-owned assignment definition that links one initial `Seed_Strategy` to one versioned `Growth_Strategy` and declares the profile's subscriptions and reserve/cap settings; the assigned `Growth_Strategy` owns the ordered mechanisms, triggers, eligibility rules, priorities, and pacing limits.
- **Seed_Strategy** — an initial roster-shape definition used only by `Seed_Operation`; it does not define a generated stable's final roster size or post-seed growth policy.
- **Solo_Specialist** — a `Seed_Strategy` with exactly 1 initial robot.
- **Pair_Team** — a `Seed_Strategy` with exactly 2 initial robots.
- **Trio_Formation** — a `Seed_Strategy` with exactly 3 initial robots.
- **Growth_Strategy** — a separate versioned, server-owned post-seed policy containing ordered growth mechanisms, triggers, eligibility rules, priorities, and pacing limits; a `Growth_Strategy` may permit robot, facility, weapon, refinement, and `Robot_Acquisition` actions.
- **Robot_Acquisition** — a balance-funded purchase of an additional robot through the existing robot-creation domain path; a `Robot_Acquisition` is permitted only when the assigned `Growth_Strategy` enables it.
- **Seed_Operation** — an idempotent lifecycle operation that creates profile-defined generated accounts and their initial assets, assignments, and approved starting forms.
- **Opening_Balance_Boundary** — account or season initialization that is not a current-economy transaction.
- **Preparation_Phase** — a season phase in which settlement only advances preparation and does not close a financial cycle.
- **Competitive_Phase** — a season phase in which normal battles, settlement, and generated maintenance may run.
- **Settlement_Components** — exactly one `passive_income` pair and one `operating_costs` pair for an applicable stable/cycle.
- **Maintenance_Window** — the interval after `Settlement_Components` and before `cycle_end_balance` capture.
- **Maintenance_Plan** — a deterministic zero-or-more list of affordable actions for one generated stable and cycle.
- **Upgrade_Action** — one approved robot attribute, weapon, refinement, facility upgrade, or `Robot_Acquisition` selected by a `Maintenance_Plan`.
- **Reserve_Balance** — a non-spendable balance floor retained for future mandatory costs.
- **Spendable_Balance** — the post-settlement balance above `Reserve_Balance`.
- **Pacing_Cap** — the fixed-credit, percentage, cooldown, and action-count limits that bound generated progression.
- **Automatic_Repair** — normal scheduled repair that may charge a generated stable.
- **Manual_Repair** — player-selected repair, forbidden to the maintenance program.
- **Season_Rollover** — the archive, verification, purge, and next-season-opening operation.
- **Serialized_Cycle_Cutover** — the closing-cycle claim and user-ID watermark shared by scheduled and admin settlement.
- **Financial_Pair** — one `FinancialLedger` row and one paired `AuditLog` row with `eventType` `financial_transaction` for one credit mutation.
- **Maintenance_Identity** — the deterministic idempotency identity of one generated `Upgrade_Action`, scoped to the generated stable, season, cycle, assigned `Growth_Strategy` version, and action identity.
- **Generated_Stable_Archive** — denormalized season evidence that preserves a generated stable's profile, initial `Seed_Strategy`, resulting roster, season-owned state, balances, competitive/economic records, and maintenance evidence before the live account is removed.
- **Admin_Maintenance_Control** — the authenticated admin surface for configuration, preview, seeding, disabling, and observation.
- **Maintenance_Observability** — settlement summaries, audit data, metrics, and alerts for generated maintenance.
- **Documentation_Contract** — the named guide and steering-file updates required to keep the implemented architecture discoverable.

## Introduction

ACC has fewer than 15 real users. Existing generated accounts and robots fill matchmaking slots but never improve, allowing real users to dominate the server after even modest upgrades. The `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` will add a small, server-maintained population that progresses under the same balance and accounting boundaries as real users while remaining explicitly non-human.

This specification defines the required behavior and delivery contract for profile-driven 1-, 2-, and 3-robot starting seeds, settlement-integrated growth actions, affordability/reserve rules, no-`Manual_Repair` enforcement, lifecycle preservation, admin controls, observability, tests, and documentation. It does not implement application code in this workflow step.

## Expected Contribution

1. **Replace static generated progression with bounded progression**: before this work, generated robots have no maintained growth path; after it, every enabled `Maintenance_Profile` can produce zero or more deterministic, balance-funded `Upgrade_Action` values per active settlement cycle, bounded by the assigned `Growth_Strategy` and reported with measurable counts and spend summaries.
2. **Make the three initial roster strategies operationally reproducible without fixing final roster size**: before this work, generated seeding is not defined as maintained 1/2/3-robot starting cohorts; after it, `Solo_Specialist`, `Pair_Team`, and `Trio_Formation` have exact starting cardinality, deterministic profile identity, idempotent lifecycle creation, and independently configurable post-seed `Growth_Strategy` mechanisms, including optional `Robot_Acquisition`.
3. **Keep generated spending inside financial evidence**: before this work, there is no generated growth capture contract; after it, every charged growth action, including permitted robot acquisition, uses an allowed existing taxonomy and exactly one `Financial_Pair`, with no direct `User.currency` writer or recurring stipend.
4. **Prevent generated progression from matching human control**: before this work, there is no reserve, cap, or repair boundary; after it, generated actions are limited by `Reserve_Balance`, `Pacing_Cap`, post-settlement affordability, profile-specific growth eligibility, and a hard prohibition on `Manual_Repair`, while normal `Automatic_Repair` remains separate.
5. **Preserve lifecycle and operator safety**: before this work, generated maintenance has no documented relationship to settlement cutover or rollover; after it, preparation produces no maintenance, final-cycle maintenance precedes archive, every generated stable's season-owned state is archived before live removal, the new season idempotently reseeds fresh `Solo_Specialist`, `Pair_Team`, and `Trio_Formation` starting forms before any post-seed growth, and admins can preview/disable without an off-cycle growth-action button.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Generated_Stable Identity and Separation

**User Story:** As an operator, I want maintained accounts to be unambiguously generated so that real users are never modified by bot maintenance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a maintenance candidate is selected for a maintenance attempt, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL read `User.isGenerated` from the current persisted account state inside the transaction that could perform any maintenance mutation, admit the candidate to maintained processing only when the value is exactly `true`, and SHALL never derive the classification from a username prefix or another derived value.
2. WHEN a candidate is identified as a `Human_Stable`, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL apply no generated maintenance to that candidate: zero generated `Upgrade_Action` values, zero generated growth-action mutations, and zero generated-profile changes; ordinary settlement actions SHALL remain governed by the existing settlement rules.
3. WHEN a generated account is processed and its maintained profile assignment is unassigned, undefined, disabled, or invalid for that account, THE system SHALL complete non-generated settlement, record exactly one `'not_configured'` skip result for that account during that maintenance run, select no fallback profile, and perform zero generated growth-action mutations.
4. IF a `Human_Stable` has a maintained profile assignment, THEN THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL emit exactly one integrity signal identifying the invalid assignment for that maintenance attempt, perform zero generated `Upgrade_Action` values and zero other generated-maintenance mutations for the account, and preserve the account's robot, facility, balance, and profile-assignment state with respect to generated maintenance.
5. WHEN a generated account has no maintained assignment, THE system SHALL continue processing it under the existing generic-generated-bot rules without failing solely because the assignment is absent, SHALL preserve its generic-bot classification, and SHALL create, infer, or silently convert neither a maintained assignment nor a `Maintenance_Profile`.

### Requirement 2: Initial Seed_Strategy Variants, Growth_Strategy, and Seed_Operation

**User Story:** As an operator, I want reproducible initial 1-, 2-, and 3-robot generated stables with distinct controlled growth policies, so that I can tune population shape without manually creating accounts or fixing a final roster size.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL define exactly three `Seed_Strategy` profiles for initial seeding: `Solo_Specialist` with exactly 1 initial robot, `Pair_Team` with exactly 2 initial robots, and `Trio_Formation` with exactly 3 initial robots, and SHALL reject every other profile name for an initial seed request.
2. THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL define `Growth_Strategy` separately from `Seed_Strategy`, SHALL version every `Growth_Strategy`, and SHALL allow each of the three seed profiles to select its own versioned `Growth_Strategy` with an ordered mechanism list, triggers, eligibility rules, priorities, and pacing limits; different seed profiles SHALL be able to select different mechanisms and policies, and no `Seed_Strategy` SHALL define a final roster size.
3. THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL support, at minimum, growth mechanisms for upgrading existing robots, facilities, weapons, and refinements, and a profile-controlled `Robot_Acquisition` mechanism for purchasing additional robots; each profile SHALL be able to permit or omit each mechanism, and no profile SHALL be required to purchase robots or have a fixed final roster count.
4. WHEN a `Seed_Operation` completes successfully, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL create exactly the requested number of stables for each requested profile, SHALL give every created stable exactly the profile's declared initial robot count, SHALL assign the selected versioned `Growth_Strategy`, and SHALL create every profile-declared required subscription, weapon, facility, and team association in a valid state.
5. WHEN a `Seed_Operation` is repeated or retried with the same `(season, profile, ordinal)` identity, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL preserve the original successfully seeded stable and its associated resources, SHALL create no duplicate stable account, robot, team, subscription, weapon, facility, assignment, or opening asset, and SHALL leave the first successful seed's state unchanged.
6. WHEN a `Seed_Operation` creates a generated stable, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL record that stable's opening currency and starter assets as one `Opening_Balance_Boundary` and SHALL emit zero `passive_income`, `battle_income`, `settlement_adjustment`, or recurring subsidy events for that opening.
7. WHEN a generated stable is evaluated during an eligible `Maintenance_Window`, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL evaluate the assigned `Growth_Strategy` triggers, priorities, eligibility rules, capacity, `Reserve_Balance`, and affordability before selecting zero or more `Upgrade_Action` values, SHALL permit zero actions when a trigger does not fire or when eligibility, capacity, reserve, or affordability blocks growth, SHALL permit a `Robot_Acquisition` only when the assigned `Growth_Strategy` enables it, and SHALL execute no growth action outside settlement.
8. WHEN a `Robot_Acquisition` is selected, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL use the existing robot-creation domain validation path, SHALL require available roster capacity and valid team and subscription prerequisites, SHALL fund the purchase from the generated stable's balance under the stable's reserve and affordability rules, SHALL route the charge through `Credit_Mutation_Service` with taxonomy `robot_creation`, SHALL execute the acquisition only during the settlement `Maintenance_Window`, and SHALL apply no acquisition when any validation, prerequisite, reserve, or affordability check fails.
9. WHEN a generated growth action is evaluated or replayed, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL derive a deterministic `Maintenance_Identity` from the generated stable, season, cycle, assigned `Growth_Strategy` version, and action identity, SHALL commit at most one successful result for that identity, SHALL preserve state on an unchanged replay, and SHALL carry no growth-action identity, pending action, or growth state across `Season_Rollover`.
10. IF a seed request contains a robot count different from the selected profile's declared initial count, a non-integer or negative requested count, an inactive season, an undefined profile, a team or subscription combination that does not match the selected profile's prerequisites, or a lifecycle state that is not an approved seeding boundary, THEN THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL reject the request with an error indicating the invalid condition, SHALL create no account or associated seed resource, and SHALL leave all pre-existing accounts and resources unchanged.

### Requirement 3: Settlement_Components and Maintenance_Window Ordering

**User Story:** As an economy operator, I want generated growth actions to be part of settlement so that their balance, accounting, and cycle snapshot agree.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the `Settlement_Service` is settling an applicable stable for a cycle in `Competitive_Phase`, THE `Settlement_Service` SHALL apply exactly one `Settlement_Components` set in this order: `passive_income`, then `operating_costs`, and SHALL open the `Maintenance_Window` only after both components have completed.
2. WHEN the `Maintenance_Window` opens for a generated stable, THE `Settlement_Service` SHALL evaluate that stable's `Maintenance_Plan` using the balance resulting from both `Settlement_Components`, SHALL apply every charge for the committed generated growth actions before capturing `cycle_end_balance`, and SHALL include those same committed charges in the stable's `CycleSnapshot`.
3. WHEN a generated stable's `Maintenance_Plan` is evaluated for a cycle, THE `Settlement_Service` SHALL lock and re-read the stable's current balance and growth-action facts before selecting actions, and SHALL commit the `Settlement_Components`, selected generated growth actions, and required financial and cycle-close evidence as one atomic outcome for that stable and cycle.
4. WHEN scheduled settlement or admin bulk settlement is initiated, THE settlement operation SHALL use the same `Settlement_Service` maintenance path, ordering, and atomicity rules, and no route, cron job, or post-close hook SHALL apply a generated growth action or its financial charge independently of that path.
5. WHEN a generated stable is created after the `Serialized_Cycle_Cutover` watermark for the closing cycle, THE `Settlement_Service` SHALL exclude that stable from the closing settlement cohort, SHALL apply no same-cycle `Maintenance_Window` action to it, and SHALL leave the already-closing cohort's balance and cycle-close evidence unchanged.
6. WHILE settlement is in `Preparation_Phase`, THE settlement operation SHALL perform only preparation advancement and SHALL write none of the following: `Settlement_Components`, generated growth actions, financial settlement evidence, cycle-close evidence, or a competitive-cycle increment.
7. IF any `Settlement_Component`, selected generated growth action, or required financial or cycle-close evidence cannot be committed, THEN THE `Settlement_Service` SHALL roll back the affected stable's `Settlement_Components`, generated growth actions, balance changes, and related evidence, SHALL report a settlement failure indication, SHALL leave the cycle incomplete and marked failed, and SHALL prevent `Season_Rollover` from beginning until a complete successful settlement has committed.

### Requirement 4: Reserve_Balance, Affordability, and Pacing_Cap

**User Story:** As a balance designer, I want generated stables to grow slowly from their remaining money so that they provide competition without becoming subsidized super-users.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN post-settlement accounting for a generated stable and cycle completes, THE maintenance controller SHALL calculate `Reserve_Balance` by applying the active `Maintenance_Profile` version's documented hard-floor and mandatory-cost reserve rule, SHALL ensure that `Reserve_Balance` is greater than or equal to zero, and SHALL calculate `Spendable_Balance` as `max(0, post-settlement balance - Reserve_Balance)` using the stable balance's monetary precision.
2. WHEN the maintenance controller evaluates a candidate action for application, THE maintenance controller SHALL validate the candidate's actual charge before selecting it, SHALL fund the charge only from the generated stable's available balance, and SHALL reject the candidate without changing its balance or target state if applying the selected actions would make the closing balance less than `Reserve_Balance` or less than zero.
3. THE maintenance controller SHALL ensure that `Total_Action_Spend`, defined as the sum of the validated charges for all selected actions in the stable's cycle plan, is no greater than the minimum of `Spendable_Balance` and every applicable configured monetary cap, including the fixed per-cycle cap, the percentage cap calculated from the post-settlement balance, and any action-specific monetary cap; THE maintenance controller SHALL reject any configuration with a negative monetary cap or a percentage cap outside the inclusive range from 0% through 100%.
4. WHEN a maintenance plan is generated, THE maintenance controller SHALL source the candidate set, trigger decisions, eligibility rules, priorities, deterministic evaluation order, and action-count limit exclusively from the assigned `Growth_Strategy` version; THE maintenance controller SHALL apply reserve, cap, affordability, and capacity rules to every candidate and SHALL select no more than one meaningful `Upgrade_Action` per stable per cycle, where a meaningful `Upgrade_Action` changes its target state and has a validated charge, unless the assigned `Growth_Strategy` contains a configured finite integer action-count limit of at least 2 for that cycle; WHEN that limit applies, THE maintenance controller SHALL select no more than the configured limit of meaningful `Upgrade_Action` values.
5. IF the profile is disabled, available cycle budget is zero, an active cooldown blocks every candidate, the candidate set is empty, or every candidate is unaffordable, has an invalid target, or fails an eligibility condition, THEN the maintenance controller SHALL return a no-op `Maintenance_Plan` containing a typed skip reason for each applicable blocking condition, SHALL apply no balance or target-state mutation, and SHALL not automatically retry or re-evaluate that plan during the same cycle.
6. THE maintenance controller SHALL not issue recurring credits, performance catch-up grants, negative-cost growth actions, or admin-funded balance adjustments, and SHALL not apply a meaningful `Upgrade_Action` unless its validated charge is greater than zero and funded from the generated stable's balance.
7. WHEN the active `Maintenance_Profile` version, assigned `Growth_Strategy` version, cycle, generated-stable facts, and post-settlement balance are equal, THE maintenance controller SHALL produce the same ordered `Maintenance_Plan`, including action selection, validated amounts, skip reasons, and plan identity, regardless of invocation time or invocation order.
8. IF the validated actual action cost differs at the stable balance's monetary precision from the immutable amount recorded in the `Maintenance_Plan`, THEN the stable transaction SHALL fail closed with a failure indication identifying the cost mismatch, SHALL preserve the stable balance and all target states, and SHALL apply no action from that plan or automatically retry it.

### Requirement 5: Credit Evidence and Repair Boundaries

**User Story:** As a finance and repair auditor, I want generated actions to use existing accounting and repair boundaries so that generated activity cannot bypass evidence or manual-repair rules.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a generated stable attempts to execute a charged robot attribute, facility, weapon, refinement, or `Robot_Acquisition` `Upgrade_Action`, THE generated-stable maintenance system SHALL apply the existing domain validation before charging, SHALL charge only after that validation succeeds, and SHALL route the charge through `Credit_Mutation_Service` using exactly one of these taxonomy values: `attribute_upgrade`, `facility_upgrade`, `weapon_purchase`, `weapon_refinement`, or `robot_creation`.
2. WHEN a charged `Upgrade_Action` completes successfully, THE generated-stable maintenance system SHALL create exactly one `Financial_Pair` for that action, consisting of exactly one `FinancialLedger` record and one matching `AuditLog` record with `eventType` `financial_transaction`; both records SHALL share one non-null `financialEventId`, and the stored breakdown SHALL include the `Maintenance_Profile` identity and version, assigned `Growth_Strategy` identity and version, season and cycle, target, reserve and cap inputs, formula version, rounding rule, and final amount, with each value matching the charged action.
3. WHEN generated-stable maintenance performs a growth action, THE generated-stable maintenance system SHALL change `User.currency` only through `Credit_Mutation_Service` and SHALL emit none of the following taxonomy values for that growth action: `subscription_cost`, `prestige_award`, or `settlement_adjustment`.
4. WHEN a maintained generated stable requires repair during normal scheduled event processing, THE generated-stable maintenance system SHALL process that repair as `Automatic_Repair`, SHALL source repair spend and its subtype from the `robot_repair` audit evidence, SHALL keep the repair charge and evidence separate from maintenance spend, and SHALL neither invoke nor produce `Manual_Repair` for the maintenance operation.
5. IF the controller selects `robots.repairQuoteCredits`, a `battle_complete` payload, or a subtype-losing ledger total as repair spend, attempts to use any repair-spend amount—including amount evidenced by `robot_repair`—as a growth-action funding source, or receives a `Manual_Repair` request for the maintenance operation, THEN the generated-stable maintenance system SHALL reject the operation with a failure indication identifying the invalid source or operation, SHALL preserve the pre-operation balance and selected action state, and SHALL create no charge, growth action, repair-spend attribution, or financial evidence for that operation.
6. WHEN the same stable/cycle/action identity is replayed after a successful execution with unchanged owner, target, amount, taxonomy, and breakdown, THE generated-stable maintenance system SHALL leave the balance, selected action state, and maintenance evidence unchanged from the first execution and SHALL not create a second charge or `Financial_Pair`.
7. IF a previously accepted stable/cycle/action identity is submitted with a changed owner, target, amount, taxonomy, or breakdown, THEN the generated-stable maintenance system SHALL reject the submission with a failure indication identifying the identity conflict, SHALL preserve the balance, selected action state, and prior maintenance evidence, and SHALL not create a charge, growth action, or `Financial_Pair`.
8. IF any `Financial_Pair`, domain update, or maintenance-evidence write fails during one stable's settlement, THEN the generated-stable maintenance system SHALL roll back that stable's settlement components, balance change, selected growth actions, maintenance record, and any partial accounting or audit evidence together, SHALL leave no partial result committed, and SHALL report the settlement failure.

### Requirement 6: Season_Rollover and Lifecycle Preservation

**User Story:** As a season operator, I want generated maintenance to respect existing season boundaries so that no old bot state leaks into a new competition.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN settlement is triggered for the `Competitive_Phase` cycle designated as the season's final cycle, THE System SHALL complete, in order, all applicable `Settlement_Components`, all applicable generated-maintenance actions in the `Maintenance_Window`, closing-balance and snapshot capture, and cycle completion, and SHALL permit `Season_Rollover` to begin only after every listed step has completed successfully.
2. IF any required final `Competitive_Phase` settlement action fails or remains incomplete, THEN THE System SHALL not begin `Season_Rollover`, SHALL report a transition failure identifying the incomplete final settlement, and SHALL preserve all unarchived live season state and retryable settlement evidence without applying archive, purge, reset, or reseed actions.
3. WHEN `Season_Rollover` archives a `Generated_Stable`, THE System SHALL persist the complete generated season state—including profile identity and assigned `Seed_Strategy`, initial roster cardinality, complete resulting roster and permitted growth state, opening and closing balances, robots, facilities, profile assignments, maintenance records, competitive and economic records, and maintenance evidence—in `Generated_Stable_Archive` or the existing denormalized season evidence, and SHALL remove the corresponding live `Generated_Stable` account and associated live season state only after that archive persistence succeeds.
4. IF persistence of the complete generated season state in `Generated_Stable_Archive` or the existing denormalized season evidence fails, THEN THE System SHALL retain the live `Generated_Stable` account and its associated live season state, SHALL perform no partial live removal, `Human_Stable` reset, or new-season reseed, and SHALL report the rollover as failed.
5. WHEN `Season_Rollover` processes a `Human_Stable`, THE System SHALL apply every action defined by the existing human reset contract, SHALL not apply `Generated_Stable` archive-and-reseed behavior, and SHALL preserve `User.isGenerated=false` before and after the rollover.
6. WHEN `Season_Rollover` has completed successfully and the approved new-season `Seed_Operation` is initiated, THE `Generated_Stable_Maintenance_Program` SHALL idempotently reseed fresh `Generated_Stable` accounts from the approved `Solo_Specialist`, `Pair_Team`, and `Trio_Formation` profiles; every resulting stable SHALL begin with exactly 1, 2, or 3 initial robots according to its selected profile, have the approved new-season opening currency and starter assets, and receive new-season profile assignments, versioned `Growth_Strategy` assignments, and `Maintenance_Identity` values; THE program SHALL not reuse any prior-season account or associated resource IDs or carry forward any prior-season balances, growth actions, `Maintenance_Identity` values, or live season state, and any later roster growth SHALL begin only through the new assignment's policy.
7. WHEN season history for a `Generated_Stable` removed by `Season_Rollover` is requested, THE System SHALL source the profile identity, assigned `Seed_Strategy`, initial roster cardinality, resulting roster, opening and closing balances, competitive and economic records, and maintenance evidence from `Generated_Stable_Archive` or the existing denormalized season evidence, and SHALL not require the purged live account or live season state.
8. WHEN a `Season_Rollover`, settlement, admin or scheduled maintenance operation, or new-season `Seed_Operation` is retried or invoked concurrently for the same season and applicable stable, cycle, `Maintenance_Identity`, or `(season, profile, ordinal)` identity, THE System SHALL commit at most one successful result for that identity, SHALL create no duplicate archive representation, settlement result, seeded account, associated resource set, reset outcome, or completed maintenance action, and SHALL leave state and evidence unchanged for every later or redundant invocation.

### Requirement 7: Admin_Maintenance_Control and Maintenance_Observability

**User Story:** As an administrator, I want to inspect and safely tune generated populations without applying hidden off-cycle growth actions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an authenticated administrator opens the admin surface, THE admin surface SHALL display controls for status, profile configuration, seed preview, seed confirmation, next-settlement dry-run, profile disable, and generated-only observability; status SHALL display the enabled or disabled state of every defined `Maintenance_Profile` and the active profile and version; and seed preview and next-settlement dry-run SHALL leave configuration, generated populations, balances, and settlement state unchanged after completion.
2. THE admin surface SHALL not expose a `runUpgradeNow` action, SHALL not execute, schedule, queue, or retry a generated growth action outside a settlement `Maintenance_Window`, and SHALL leave generated population, balance, profile, settlement, and growth-action state unchanged when no settlement `Maintenance_Window` is in progress.
3. WHEN an administrator submits one profile configuration, seed preview, seed confirmation, or profile-disable request, THE system SHALL record exactly one admin audit entry for that request containing the actor, active season, profile identifier and version, supplied requested counts or caps or an explicit not-applicable value, operation type, preview or confirmation result or an explicit not-applicable value, and, when the request fails, the failure outcome and violation category.
4. WHEN a settlement completes for a generated-maintenance run or an administrator requests maintenance analytics, THE system SHALL report, for the corresponding settlement execution and selected population scope, the counts of generated stables considered, growth actions selected, and stables skipped; counts for every defined profile variant and `Upgrade_Action` kind, including `Robot_Acquisition`; total Credits spent; reserve-blocked count; invalid-profile count; opening and closing balances; duration in milliseconds; and integrity-alert counts for each violation category; every count and total SHALL be reported as zero when no corresponding event occurred, and player-visible analytics SHALL omit raw internal financial identities.
5. WHEN an administrator selects generated-only, human-only, or all-user analytics, THE system SHALL classify users solely by `User.isGenerated`: generated-only results SHALL include every matching user with `User.isGenerated=true` and exclude every user with `User.isGenerated=false`; human-only results SHALL include every matching user with `User.isGenerated=false` and exclude every user with `User.isGenerated=true`; and all-user results SHALL include matching users with either value, including a `false`-valued user whose username resembles a bot prefix in human-only results.
6. WHILE the admin UI viewport is between 320 and 1023 pixels wide inclusive, THE admin UI SHALL stack sections vertically, SHALL have no horizontal overflow, SHALL keep all controls and labels operable using vertical scrolling only, and SHALL provide every touch target with a hit area of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
7. IF the system detects a human selected for a generated population, an invalid assignment, a `Manual_Repair` attempt against a generated stable, a direct balance writer, an off-cycle charge or growth-action attempt, a cap or reserve violation, a conflicting `Maintenance_Identity`, or a population-count invariant failure, THEN the system SHALL reject or halt the affected operation before it completes, roll back any partial business-state changes from that operation, report a failure indication, leave no balance, profile, population, settlement, or growth-action state changed by the failed operation, and issue an administrator-visible alert identifying every applicable violation category.
8. IF a requester is not an authenticated administrator, THEN the admin surface SHALL withhold the protected actions named in criterion 1, provide an access-denied failure indication, and leave profile, seed, settlement, population, balance, and observability data unchanged.
9. WHEN an authenticated administrator submits a valid seed confirmation while the lifecycle is marked as an approved boundary, THE system SHALL apply the seed confirmation, commit all requested seed results and assignments, and report successful completion only after those results are complete.
10. IF submitted profile or seed values fail validation, or seed confirmation is requested while the lifecycle is not marked as an approved boundary, THEN the admin surface SHALL reject the request before applying a mutation, report a failure indication identifying the invalid condition, preserve the prior configuration, population, balance, and settlement state, and record the rejection in the single admin audit entry required by criterion 3.

### Requirement 8: Testing and Documentation_Contract

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want executable proof and current documentation so that future changes cannot silently bypass the generated-stable boundaries.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the unit-test suite is executed, THE unit-test suite SHALL assert pure budget and cap calculations at the zero, configured-maximum, and one-unit-over-maximum boundaries; validate every valid and invalid `Seed_Strategy` and `Growth_Strategy` form defined by the profile rules; validate at least two distinct ordered `Growth_Strategy` mechanism lists; accept exactly 1, 2, and 3 initial robots for the three seed profiles while rejecting 0 initial robots and every initial count greater than 3; validate permitted and omitted growth mechanisms, including enabled and omitted `Robot_Acquisition`, `Robot_Acquisition` domain prerequisites, and `robot_creation` taxonomy; verify that at least one permitted `Robot_Acquisition` grows a roster beyond its initial cardinality without imposing a fixed final roster count; produce identical deterministic ordering, including tied inputs, for identical inputs; return the defined typed skip reason and perform no growth action for each defined skip condition, including a disabled profile, zero cycle budget, a cooldown blocking every candidate, an empty candidate set, and candidates that are unaffordable, invalid, capacity-blocked, or ineligible; and reject identity conflicts and `Manual_Repair` inputs without mutating balances, profiles, or maintenance state.
2. WHEN the fast-check property-test suite is executed, THE fast-check property-test suite SHALL generate valid and invalid generated-stable balances and profile inputs and assert that every generated case preserves the configured reserve, never produces a negative balance, never exceeds the configured spend budget or action cap, exercises at least two distinct ordered `Growth_Strategy` mechanism lists with both enabled and omitted `Robot_Acquisition`, leaves the resulting state unchanged when the same growth action is retried, rejects identity conflicts without mutation, leaves `Human_Stable` state unchanged, performs no balance, profile, or maintenance change during `Preparation_Phase`, permits a valid acquisition case to grow a roster beyond its initial cardinality without assuming a fixed final roster count, and prevents any pre-rollover balance, profile, growth action, or identity input from changing the subsequently seeded season's initial state.
3. WHEN PostgreSQL integration tests are executed, THE PostgreSQL integration-test suite SHALL verify that scheduled and admin settlement paths use the same ordering of `passive_income`, `operating_costs`, and `Maintenance_Window`; each financial mutation, including `Robot_Acquisition`, produces exactly one corresponding `Financial_Pair`; the closing balance includes every mutation committed before closure and excludes every mutation committed after closure; a forced transactional failure leaves no partial balance, growth action, or financial records; a seed created after the `Serialized_Cycle_Cutover` watermark receives no growth action in that same cycle; and final-cycle settlement and `Season_Rollover` each occur once without reapplying that cycle's growth action or balance afterward.
4. WHEN heavy tests are executed, THE heavy-test suite SHALL run at least 3 consecutive cycles for each of the 3 `Seed_Strategy` variants and SHALL verify for every stable and cycle that spend does not exceed the configured budget, no balance becomes negative, no duplicate `Maintenance_Identity` occurs across cycles or retries, the generated-stable account count remains unchanged except for approved seed operations, at least one permitted `Robot_Acquisition` grows a roster beyond its initial cardinality, every roster change occurs only through a validated permitted `Robot_Acquisition`, no fixed final roster count is assumed, and settlement duration in milliseconds is no greater than the settlement-performance threshold defined by the design.
5. WHEN the implementation is finalized, THE documentation set SHALL update every document named in the design's Documentation Impact section, including `.kiro/steering/project-overview.md`, `.kiro/steering/testing-strategy.md`, `.kiro/steering/monitoring-observability.md`, `.kiro/steering/coding-standards.md`, `docs/README.md`, `docs/guides/FINANCIAL_LEDGER_AUDIT_GUIDE.md`, `docs/guides/ADMIN_PANEL_GUIDE.md`, `docs/guides/GENERATED_STABLE_MAINTENANCE_GUIDE.md`, `docs/game-systems/PRD_CYCLE_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/game-systems/PRD_SEASON_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/game-systems/PRD_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md`, and `docs/architecture/PRD_SERVICE_DIRECTORY.md`; each named document SHALL contain no statement that generated accounts are reset as `Human_Stable` accounts, removed without archive-before-removal, or reseeded without fresh new-season state.
6. WHEN the final verification task is executed, THE final verification task SHALL run each of the 6 numbered aggregate checks listed under `Verification Criteria` in `Expected Contribution` and SHALL record each check's outcome as passed, failed, or blocked; a blocked check SHALL include the reason it could not run, and no check SHALL be recorded as passed unless that check completed successfully.
