## Credits

All Grand Melee participants earn credits. The base payout is **2.5× the league tier base** multiplied by a placement multiplier. Higher placements earn significantly more credits.

Even robots that finish in the bottom half receive a **last-place floor** — you'll never walk away empty-handed.

## Fame

**Top 10 finishers** earn Fame from Grand Melee. Fame rewards are **tier-scaled** — placing well in higher tiers earns more Fame than the same placement in lower tiers.

Robots finishing 11th–20th do not earn Fame from that battle.

## Prestige

**Top 10 finishers** earn Prestige, the same places that earn Fame and LP. Prestige is **tier-scaled**. The winner receives by far the largest award, and the amounts fall off steeply after 3rd.

```callout-info
Until November 2026 only the top 3 earned Prestige. With fields of 16–20 robots that paid about one robot in six; places 4–10 now earn a smaller share as well.
```

### Winner's Bonus

The 1st place finisher receives a **+50% Prestige bonus** if they end the battle with more than 50% HP remaining. Dominating a 20-robot free-for-all while staying healthy is the ultimate display of superiority.

## Reward Summary

| Placement | Credits | Fame | Prestige |
|-----------|---------|------|----------|
| 1st | 2.5× base × top multiplier | ✅ Tier-scaled | ✅ + 50% HP bonus if >50% HP |
| 2nd–3rd | 2.5× base × placement multiplier | ✅ Tier-scaled | ✅ Tier-scaled |
| 4th–10th | 2.5× base × placement multiplier | ✅ Tier-scaled | ✅ Tier-scaled (small) |
| 11th–20th | 2.5× base × low multiplier (floor) | — | — |

## Last-Place Floor

Every robot that **fights** in a Grand Melee receives at least the floor credit reward regardless of placement. This ensures that even a last-place finish contributes to your economic progress.

```callout-warning
Don't confuse this with a **bye**. The last-place floor is what you earn for turning up and finishing last — 2.5× the league tier base times the lowest placement multiplier. A [bye](/guide/economy/battle-rewards) is what you earn when the field was too thin to run at all, and it pays the ordinary participation reward for your tier, which is a good deal less. Fighting and finishing last pays more than not fighting.
```
