A **season** is a bounded competitive period. It runs for 100 cycles — 100 days — and then it ends. When it ends, everything you built during that season is archived and then deleted, and everyone starts the next season from the same position.

## Why seasons exist

Without them, the game is one endless timeline. Credits pile up with nothing left worth buying, facility levels only ever go up, and a player who joined last week is permanently months behind one who joined at launch. A veteran's advantage compounds forever, and your own choices stop mattering once everything is maxed.

Seasons fix that by making the clock run out. Every economic decision is scarce again, because you are always rebuilding from starting credits. Every season opens on a shifted meta, because balance changes land at the boundary. And a new player joining on cycle 3 of a season is genuinely in the same race as everyone else.

## The shape of a season

A season has two parts:

**Preparation** — a short window, normally 2 cycles, with no scheduled battles at all. This is when you buy robots and weapons, build facilities, allocate attributes and tuning, form teams, and subscribe to events. Nothing you do here can cost you a battle, because none are running.

**Competitive** — 100 cycles of normal play. Leagues, tournaments, King of the Hill, team battles, and Grand Melee all run on their usual daily schedule.

The very first competitive cycle is a scheduling cycle. Each battle event executes the matches that were scheduled the previous day before scheduling tomorrow's, so on cycle 1 there is nothing yet due to run. Your first battles happen on cycle 2. This is expected, not a fault.

## Where you are right now

The season indicator in the navigation always shows the current season and cycle, reading `Season 3 · Cycle 42 / 100`. During preparation it shows the preparation day instead. Your Dashboard shows how many cycles remain.

In the final 7 cycles of a season a banner appears warning you that the reset is coming, so you can spend down credits, chase a last promotion, or push for a record before the boundary.

## Season 0

If you have been playing since before seasons existed, your history sits in **Season 0**. That is the open-ended timeline that ran before this system, and it has no fixed length — it ends when an administrator closes it. Because it predates seasons, its figures are career totals rather than one season's numbers, and the game labels them that way wherever they appear.
