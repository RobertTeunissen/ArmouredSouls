Nothing you achieved is lost at a reset — it moves. Your season archive is permanent and there are two places to read it.

## Your stable page

Your stable page carries a **season history** block. The season in progress is at the top, then one row per completed season with its prestige, championship titles, best tier reached, and how many cycles it ran. The most recent completed season opens automatically.

An open season reads like the stable page did at the time:

- **Headline figures**: prestige earned, final credits, win-loss-draw record, win rate, highest ELO, titles, and achievements
- **Every robot you fielded**, with its highest tier over every mode and a strip showing its tier in each mode (1v1, 2v2, 3v3, Tag Team, King of the Hill, Grand Melee). Hover or focus a cell for the League Points and rank in that league instance. Team modes show the robot's team standing.
- **Each robot's best finish**: its highest tier, and its rank there
- **Teams**, with their members
- **Hall of Records placements** your stable earned that season, plus its tournament wins

A rank here is your true position in that league instance, counting every competitor including the system-generated stables. If you finished fourth, it says fourth.

## The Season Archive page

The Season Archive at `/seasons` is the shared view: every completed season, its final standings in every mode, its champions, and the records it produced. The records are laid out like the live Hall of Records, one tile per record with its holder and value, and each opens its full top 10. Your own places are marked and your stable name links to your stable page.

System-generated stables appear here and are labelled as such. They are not hidden, because they hold many league positions — filtering them out would misrepresent where players actually finished.

## What an accolade is

An accolade is a frozen Hall of Records placement. The Hall of Records is a live view of the current season, so when a season ends its rankings would otherwise vanish along with the battle data behind them. At the boundary the top placements of every category are copied into permanent records: most damage in a single battle, biggest upset, longest win streak, career and economic records, King of the Hill and Grand Melee placements, and tournament champions. The Hall of Records itself only shows the season in progress; the Season Archive is where earlier seasons' records live.

They read like "3rd most kills in 1v1 League" — a specific, permanent claim about what your stable was known for that season.

## Season 0 is different

Season 0 predates the season system, so no baseline exists for figures like prestige earned or battles won *during* that season. Those are career totals accumulated before seasons existed, and the game labels them that way rather than pretending they describe a single 100-cycle season.

## Images in your archive

Archived robots show the artwork they wore. If you later delete that image from your library, the archived robot falls back to a default silhouette — every other value in the archive stays exactly as it was recorded.
