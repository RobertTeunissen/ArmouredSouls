Nothing you achieved is lost at a reset — it moves. Your season archive is permanent and there are two places to read it.

## Your stable page

Your stable page carries a **season history** block: one collapsed row per completed season showing that season's final credits, prestige, win-loss-draw record, win rate, best tier reached, championship titles, and achievement count, along with how many cycles the season ran.

Expand a row and you get the detail:

- **Every robot you fielded**, with its final ELO, fame, and record
- **Where each robot finished in each mode** — tier, league instance, League Points, and its rank within that instance
- **Team memberships**, with each team's final standing per mode
- **Your accolades** — the notable placements your stable earned that season

A rank here is your true position in that league instance, counting every competitor including the system-generated stables. If you finished fourth, it says fourth.

## The Season Archive page

The Season Archive at `/seasons` is the shared view: every completed season, its final standings in every mode, its champions, and the records it produced. Stable names link through to that stable's own history.

System-generated stables appear here and are labelled as such. They are not hidden, because they hold most league positions — filtering them out would misrepresent where players actually finished.

## What an accolade is

An accolade is a frozen Hall of Records placement. The Hall of Records is a live view of the current season, so when a season ends its rankings would otherwise vanish along with the battle data behind them. At the boundary the top placements of every category are copied into permanent records: most damage in a single battle, narrowest victory, longest win streak, career and economic records, King of the Hill and Grand Melee placements, team battle records, and tournament champions.

They read like "3rd most kills in 1v1 League" — a specific, permanent claim about what your stable was known for that season.

## Season 0 is different

Season 0 predates the season system, so no baseline exists for figures like prestige earned or battles won *during* that season. Those are career totals accumulated before seasons existed, and the game labels them that way rather than pretending they describe a single 100-cycle season.

## Images in your archive

Archived robots show the artwork they wore. If you later delete that image from your library, the archived robot falls back to a default silhouette — every other value in the archive stays exactly as it was recorded.
