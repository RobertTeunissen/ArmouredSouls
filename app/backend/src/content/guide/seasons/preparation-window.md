Between seasons there is a **preparation window** — normally 2 cycles — with no scheduled combat of any kind.

## Why it exists

Without it, a reset would drop you into a league day with no robots. You would forfeit battles through no fault of your own, and you would likely miss the first tournament bracket entirely because it fills before you have finished rebuilding.

The preparation window removes that risk completely. Every battle event is suspended: 1v1 league, tournaments, tag team, King of the Hill, 2v2 and 3v3 league, team tournaments, and Grand Melee. No matches are scheduled, none are executed, and no tournament brackets are created.

## What you can do

Everything to do with building:

- Create robots
- Buy, refine, and sell weapons
- Buy and upgrade facilities
- Upgrade attributes and allocate tuning points
- Change loadouts and stances
- Create teams and change their membership
- Subscribe robots to events through the Booking Office
- Run Practice Arena battles to test builds

Subscriptions you set during preparation persist, so your robots are eligible for matchmaking on the first competitive cycle. You do not need to wait and re-do them.

## What does not happen

Because no economy runs during preparation, you get no passive income and pay no operating costs. The global cycle counter does not advance either, so preparation cycles are not counted as game cycles in your financial history.

Your Dashboard shows which preparation day it is and how many remain.

## Balance changes land here

The preparation window is where balance changes are applied. Weapon damage, facility costs, attribute prices, reward curves — anything that changes how the game plays arrives at the boundary rather than mid-season, so a change never alters the season you are currently competing in.

That means the meta you prepared for last season may not be the meta of the new one. Check the What's New feed during preparation before committing your starting credits, and use the Practice Arena to test a build before you buy into it.
