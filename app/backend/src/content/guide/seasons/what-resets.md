At a season boundary the game archives your season, then deletes almost everything. This page is the exact list, so there are no surprises.

## What is deleted

Everything you built competitively and economically:

- **Robots** — every one, along with their ELO, fame, win-loss record, kills, and damage totals
- **Weapons** — your entire inventory, including every refinement you paid for
- **Facilities** — all of them, back to level 0
- **Attributes and tuning** — fresh robots start at base values
- **Credits** — reset to ₡3,000,000 starting credits, whether you had more or less
- **Teams** — 2v2 and 3v3 teams, and their memberships
- **Event subscriptions** — you re-subscribe in the new season
- **Standings** — LP, tier, and league placement in every mode
- **Prestige** — back to zero
- **Achievements** — back to zero, recorded per season in your archive
- **Championship titles** — the counters reset
- **Battle history** — battles, battle reports, and analytics for the season

## What survives

- **Your account** — username, email, password, and role
- **Your stable name and profile settings** — visibility, notifications, theme
- **Onboarding state** — you will not be walked through the tutorial again
- **Your uploaded robot images** — see below
- **Your season archive** — permanently, for every season you have played

## Prestige and achievements reset — deliberately

This is the part players find most surprising, so it is worth being direct about the reasoning.

Prestige gates facility levels. If prestige carried forward, a veteran would start every season able to reach facility depths a newer player could not touch for weeks. That advantage would compound across seasons and never wash out. Resetting prestige makes facility depth a goal you re-earn each season, which is what keeps the competition level.

Achievements reset for a related reason: as a permanent collection they stop being meaningful after a few seasons, because everything easy is already unlocked. Per season, they become a scorecard — a record of what you managed *this time* — and your archive keeps every season's count.

## Your uploaded images are kept

If you uploaded custom artwork for a robot, the image file survives the reset even though the robot does not. It stays in your image library, capped at 20 images, and you can apply it to a new robot next season without re-uploading. Your archived seasons keep showing the right artwork too.

You can only ever see and use your own uploads. If you want to free up space, you can delete an image yourself — the game tells you first which current robots and which archived seasons use it.
