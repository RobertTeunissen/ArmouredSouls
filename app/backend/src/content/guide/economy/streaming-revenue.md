## Overview

Streaming revenue comes from broadcasting your robots' battles to fans and viewers. Unlike [merchandising](/guide/economy/merchandising) (which is stable-level and driven by Prestige), streaming income is earned **per robot, per battle**, based on each robot's own **battle history** and **Fame**, multiplied by your stable's **Streaming Studio** level.

Every robot earns streaming revenue from its very first battle, even without a Streaming Studio. The Studio only makes each broadcast pay more.

## How Streaming Revenue Works

Streaming revenue is credited right after each battle your robot fights, not during the daily settlement. Each broadcast pays a **base of ₡1,000**, scaled by two things:

1. **Audience** — how many viewers your robot draws, built from its battle count and its Fame
2. **Streaming Studio** — a multiplier for every robot in your stable

### 1. Audience: Battles and Fame

A robot's audience grows with every battle it fights and every point of Fame it earns. The two add up: every **2,000 battles** adds one more ₡1,000 base to each broadcast, and so does every **10,000 Fame**. Nothing is capped, so every battle and every Fame point keeps counting.

Every battle your robot fights builds its streaming audience, whatever the mode: 1v1 League, 2v2 and 3v3 League, Tag Team, tournaments, King of the Hill and Grand Melee all count. The **Battles** figure on a robot's profile shows this all-modes total, which is why it can be higher than wins + losses + draws: King of the Hill and Grand Melee are placement events with no win or loss.

| Robot | Audience | Per battle, no Studio |
|-------|----------|----------------------|
| Brand new (0 battles, 0 Fame) | 1.00× | ₡1,000 |
| Mid game (500 battles, 2,500 Fame) | 1.50× | ₡1,500 |
| Veteran (552 battles, 10,492 Fame) | about 2.33× | ₡2,325 |

Fame grows through battle performance. Winning battles, achieving dominant victories, and pulling off comebacks all build Fame faster than ordinary performances. See the [Fame Tiers Guide](/guide/prestige-fame/fame-tiers) for details on how Fame accumulates.

```callout-info
Fame is per-robot, not per-stable. Each robot builds its own reputation and earns its own streaming revenue from every battle it fights.
```

### 2. Streaming Studio Facility

The **Streaming Studio** adds **50% streaming revenue per level** to every robot in your stable: 1.5× at Level 1, 2× at Level 2, up to **6× at Level 10**.

| Studio Level | Multiplier | New robot | Veteran (552 battles, 10,492 Fame) |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|-----------------------------------|
| None | 1× | ₡1,000 | ₡2,325 |
| 1 | 1.5× | ₡1,500 | ₡3,487 |
| 3 | 2.5× | ₡2,500 | ₡5,813 |
| 6 | 4× | ₡4,000 | ₡9,300 |
| 9 | 5.5× | ₡5,500 | ₡12,788 |
| 10 | 6× | ₡6,000 | ₡13,951 |

The Streaming Studio has a daily operating cost (₡100 per level), so it needs enough battles across your roster to justify its upkeep.

```callout-warning
Don't build a Streaming Studio before your robots fight regularly. Each level adds half of a robot's base award per battle, so the Studio pays for itself through battle volume. A stable whose robots fight rarely earns little from it.
```

### Team Matches and Byes

- **Team matches split the award.** In Tag Team, 2v2 and 3v3 matches, each robot earns its own streaming revenue, divided by its team size. A veteran earning ₡12,788 in a 1v1 earns ₡6,394 in a Tag Team or 2v2 match and ₡4,262 in a 3v3.
- **Byes pay no streaming revenue.** A bye (walkover) is not broadcast.

## Building Streaming Revenue Over Time

Streaming income grows naturally as your robots progress:

1. **Early game** — Robots start with no battles and little Fame. Each broadcast pays about the ₡1,000 base. Focus on battle winnings.
2. **Mid game** — Robots build battle history and Fame, so every broadcast pays more. A Streaming Studio starts paying for itself once your roster fights several battles a day.
3. **Late game** — Robots with long battle histories and high Fame earn several times the base per battle, multiplied again by the Studio.

```callout-tip
Fame builds faster with impressive performances. Perfect victories (winning without taking damage) grant 2× Fame, dominating victories grant 1.5×, and comeback victories grant 1.25×. Invest in builds that can achieve these outcomes to accelerate your streaming income growth.
```

## Streaming vs Merchandising

Both are passive income streams, but they work differently:

| Aspect | Streaming | Merchandising |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Driven by** | Robot battles + Fame | Stable Prestige per robot slot |
| **Scope** | Per robot, per battle | Per stable, per cycle |
| **Requires facility** | No (Streaming Studio multiplies it) | Yes (Merchandising Hub) |
| **Activity dependent** | Yes — no battles, no income | No — paid every cycle |
| **Best for** | Rosters that fight many battles | Concentrated stables with high Prestige |

Streaming pays per robot per match, so it rewards wide, busy rosters. Merchandising pays per stable per cycle and rewards concentrated rosters. See [Daily Financial Cycle](/guide/economy/daily-financial-cycle) for a side-by-side comparison at equal purchase cost.

## Maximizing Streaming Income

- **Keep all robots enlisted** — Every battle pays a broadcast
- **Build Fame through performance** — Every 10,000 Fame adds another base award per battle
- **Upgrade the Streaming Studio** — Each level adds 50% to every robot's streaming revenue
- **Compete in more events** — Tournaments, King of the Hill and Grand Melee are broadcasts too
