## Overview

Battle winnings are your primary income source in Armoured Souls. Every league battle and tournament match generates Credits, with the amount determined by your robot's **league tier** and the result. A win in a tier pays every stable the same. Climbing to higher tiers is the single most impactful thing you can do for your stable's finances.

![League tier reward scaling](/images/guide/economy/league-tier-reward-scaling.png)

## Base Win Rewards by League Tier

Each league tier has a fixed base win reward — the amount a winner earns per battle. Losers still earn a **participation reward: 20% of that tier's base win reward**.

| League Tier | Base Win Reward | Participation Reward | Multiplier vs Bronze |
|-------------|----------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Bronze** | ₡7,500 | ₡1,500 | 1× (baseline) |
| **Silver** | ₡15,000 | ₡3,000 | 2× |
| **Gold** | ₡30,000 | ₡6,000 | 4× |
| **Platinum** | ₡60,000 | ₡12,000 | 8× |
| **Diamond** | ₡115,000 | ₡23,000 | ~15× |
| **Champion** | ₡225,000 | ₡45,000 | 30× |

```callout-tip
The jump between tiers is substantial. Promoting even one robot from Bronze to Silver doubles your base battle income from that robot. Prioritize promotion — it's the fastest way to grow your stable's wealth.
```

## How Rewards Are Determined

Every battle participant earns credits. The outcome determines how much:

- **Victory** — The base win reward for your tier plus the participation reward (a Gold win pays ₡36,000)
- **Draw** — The participation reward
- **Loss** — The participation reward (20% of your tier's base win reward)

Every robot that fights also earns [streaming revenue](/guide/economy/streaming-revenue), win or lose. It grows with the robot's battles, Fame and your Streaming Studio, so in the lower tiers it is a large part of what a loss pays.

Even a loss generates income. Your robots are professional fighters — they get paid for showing up, win or lose. But winning consistently is the path to financial growth.

```callout-info
You always earn something from every battle. A Bronze-tier loss still pays ₡1,500 in participation rewards, which helps cover basic operating costs. But a Champion-tier victory at ₡225,000 is a game-changer for your stable's finances.
```

## Byes

Sometimes the schedule has nothing for your robot to fight — an odd number of entrants in your instance, a tier too thin to run a Grand Melee field, or a tournament bracket that needs someone to skip a round. That's a **bye**, and it still pays.

A bye pays the **participation reward** for your tier — the same figure a loss pays — multiplied by the number of robots on your side, and for team modes by the 0.75 team credit-pool factor. So a 1v1 bye pays one participation reward, a 2v2 or Tag Team bye pays 1.5, and a 3v3 bye pays 2.25 (Season 2; they were two and three). A tournament bye pays whatever a loss pays for that round.

What a bye does **not** pay is prestige, fame or streaming revenue. Those are earned by fighting.

```callout-info
No combat is simulated for a bye, so your robot takes no damage and you get no repair bill from it. A walkover leaves your roster exactly as it was.
```

Byes exist so that a subscription always returns something. If you allocated a Booking Office slot to an event, a quiet day should not mean a wasted slot — see [Booking Office](/guide/facilities/booking-office).

## Prestige Doesn't Change Battle Rewards

Battle rewards depend on the tier and the result, never on your stable's Prestige. Until Season 2, 1v1 league wins paid up to 50% extra to stables with a lot of Prestige; that bonus was removed because it paid the stables already ahead more for the same win. Prestige still unlocks facility levels and raises [merchandising income](/guide/economy/merchandising).

## Maximizing Battle Income

Here are the key levers for increasing your battle earnings:

### Promote Your Robots

Each tier promotion is a massive income boost. Focus on meeting [promotion requirements](/guide/leagues/promotion-demotion) — top 10% of your instance, at least 25 LP, and 5+ cycles in the current tier.

### Run Multiple Robots

More robots means more battles per cycle, which means more total income. Three robots in Silver earn more total Credits than one robot in Gold, though the single Gold robot has lower maintenance costs.

### Win More Often

Victories pay significantly more than losses within each tier. Investing in your robots' attributes, weapons, and strategy directly translates to higher per-battle income.

## Tournament Rewards

Tournament battles use a completely different reward system from league battles. Tournament rewards are **not based on league tier** — instead they scale with tournament size (number of participants) and round progression (how deep you are in the bracket). Base credits start at ₡20,000 per win, modified by a size multiplier and a progression multiplier.

See the [Tournament Rewards Guide](/guide/tournaments/rewards) for the full breakdown of tournament-specific earnings.
