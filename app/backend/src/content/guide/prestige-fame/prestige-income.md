## Overview

Prestige unlocks facility levels, and it raises one income stream: **merchandising**. The more Prestige your stable has for each robot slot, the more your [Merchandising Hub](/guide/economy/merchandising) earns every cycle.

Prestige does **not** change what a battle pays. A win in a tier pays every stable the same, however much Prestige it has (see [Battle Rewards](/guide/economy/battle-rewards)).

![Prestige income impact](/images/guide/prestige-fame/prestige-income-impact.png)

```callout-info
Until Season 2, 1v1 league wins paid up to 50% extra to stables with a lot of Prestige. That bonus is gone: it paid the stables that were already ahead more for the same win, which made it harder for everyone else to catch up.
```

## Merchandising Revenue Scaling

Merchandising income is the Hub's base rate multiplied by **1 + Prestige per robot slot ÷ 10,000**. Prestige per slot is your Prestige divided by your roster capacity (from Roster Expansion).

| Prestige per robot slot | Merchandising income |
|------------------------|---------------------|
| 0 | Base rate |
| 5,000 | 1.5× base |
| 10,000 | 2× base |
| 25,000 | 3.5× base |
| 50,000 | 6× base |

The scaling is smooth: every point of Prestige adds a little.

```callout-info
A Level 5 Merchandising Hub (₡125,000/day base) on a one-slot roster earns ₡250,000/day at 10,000 Prestige and ₡137,500/day at 1,000 Prestige. With three robot slots the same 10,000 Prestige is 3,333 per slot, so the Hub earns about ₡166,700/day. A wider roster needs more Prestige for the same effect.
```

## Prestige Income vs Fame Income

Your stable has two reputation-driven income streams:

| Income Type | Driven By | Scope | Consistency |
|------------|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Merchandising | Prestige per robot slot | Stable-wide | Every cycle |
| Streaming revenue | Battles fought and Fame | Per robot | Per battle fought |

Merchandising rewards a concentrated stable's reputation. Streaming rewards robots that fight often and build Fame. A well-rounded stable invests in both.

```callout-tip
In the early game, focus on winning battles to build Prestige naturally. It opens facility levels (from 1,000 Prestige) and makes a Merchandising Hub worth more over time.
```
