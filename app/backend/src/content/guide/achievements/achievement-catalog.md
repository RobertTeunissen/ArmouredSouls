## Overview

Achievements are grouped into five categories. Categories are for reference only — on the achievements page, all achievements appear in a single list with filter and sort controls.

Each achievement has a pop culture reference in its name. See if you can spot them all.

Achievements marked **🏆 Trophy** are bragging rights only: they pay no credits or prestige. See [Trophies](/guide/achievements/achievement-system#trophies) for why.

## Secret Achievements

There are also **12 secret achievements** hidden throughout the game. They appear as "???" on the achievements page until you earn them. Secret achievements are triggered by unusual or unexpected gameplay moments — we won't spoil them here.

```callout-info
Secret achievements award ₡50,000 and +50 prestige by default. They're marked with a purple badge border once unlocked.
```
